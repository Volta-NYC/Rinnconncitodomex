# Image credits

The food, team, storefront, workshop, and brand artwork in this site were supplied by
Rinconcito Domex in the public Google Drive folder shared for this project. They are
stored locally in `public/images/rinconcito/` and `public/brand/`, then served through
`next/image` with responsive AVIF/WebP optimization.

The Culinary Workshops video remains hosted in the supplied public Drive and is loaded
only when its section approaches the viewport. This preserves the original asset while
keeping the page's initial transfer lightweight.
