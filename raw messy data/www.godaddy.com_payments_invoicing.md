## GoDaddy Invoicing FAQ

### What is invoicing?

Invoicing is the process of creating and sending an invoice to a customer to request payment for goods or services. An invoice is a commercial document that formalizes the agreement between the buyer and seller and serves several purposes. GoDaddy Invoicing allows you to create and send digital invoices in just a few minutes and get paid on time.

### What does invoicing software do and how can I create invoices?

An invoicing software like GoDaddy Invoicing helps businesses send invoices and accept payments efficiently. It streamlines the invoicing process, tracks payments, sends reminders for overdue invoices, and provides a secure platform for payment transactions. You can create an invoice from Commerce Home. Simply select recipients from your customer list, add details like line items, payment details, discounts, and taxes, and send in one click.

### Does GoDaddy provide free invoicing?

Yes. GoDaddy does provide free invoicing services. With the Free plan you can:

- Enjoy 2.9% \+ 30¢ per invoice card transaction, and 1% per ACH transaction.
- Send unlimited invoices via email or pay links.
- Schedule recurring invoices.
- Save customer payment card on file.
- Send automatic payment reminders.

Users can upgrade from the Free plan to the Plus plan ($9.99/mo with annual plan, or $11.99/mo if you pay month-to-month) and get everything included in the Free plan, plus:

- Discounted 2.8% \+ 30¢ per invoice card transaction, and 0.8% with a $10 maximum per ACH transaction.
- Remove generic invoice and receipt email branding.
- Add your logo to invoices and receipt emails.
- Send automated invoices from business email.
- Secure invoice checkout links hosted on your business domain.
- Set up employee roles and access permissions.

Upgrading can provide a more professional look to invoices and enhance brand recognition. The cost for a business to use GoDaddy Invoicing if upgraded will depend on the specific plan chosen.

### Which businesses need GoDaddy Invoicing?

Small businesses in service-based industries like professional services, construction, home services, health and fitness, and freelancing often have invoicing requirements. In addition, businesses that use their [POS System](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale) for their daily operations such as retailers or food and beverage businesses may also need Invoicing to bill for bulk purchases and catering services.

### Do I need templates for invoicing?

With GoDaddy Invoicing, a template is built-in and ready for you. Businesses can upgrade to the Invoicing Plus plan and have their unique brand logo displayed on the invoices and digital receipts.

### What should be included in invoices?

When invoicing, it’s important to include components like company name and brand logo, payment details, discounts, taxes, contact details, dates, and clear descriptions for services and/or products provided — with clear descriptions. And with GoDaddy Invoicing, you can effortlessly create invoices and easily select recipients from your customer list and send invoices with one click.

### How can I send my customers an invoice?

You can send invoices via email or payment link. Send one-time or recurring invoices, based on your clients’ needs, and securely store customer payment cards for more predictable cash flow.

### What payment methods can my customers use to pay an invoice?

Your customers can pay an invoice using all major credit and debit cards, and checks, as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay. For in-person and remote payments, you can use Tap to Pay on the GoDaddy Mobile App for contactless, on-site payments (no extra hardware). Plus, the Virtual Terminal facilitates remote and over-the-phone payments.

### Can I send payment receipts with GoDaddy Invoicing?

Yes, email receipts are automatically sent for all one-time and recurring payments. For recurring invoices, your customers will get a receipt for every payment from the first invoice to the final invoice.

### How quickly can I access my funds with Invoicing?

When you receive payment from your customer, you can access your funds as soon as the next business day. Or for a fee of 1.75%, get paid on the same day including weekends and holidays. [Learn more about accessing your funds with Invoicing.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-are-my-payout-options-42464)

### Does GoDaddy integrate with other accounting software?

You can [synchronize](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/news/accounting-sync-problems-solved-with-commerce-sync) your payments like QuickBooks or through third-party apps in our App Center (additional costs apply).

### Why should I choose GoDaddy Invoicing?

GoDaddy Invoicing makes it easy for you to create, send, and manage your invoices from one place. By upgrading to the Plus plan, you can replace GoDaddy branding on invoices and receipts with your own, host the invoice checkout page on your GoDaddy domain, and send invoices from your [business email](https://www.godaddy.com/email/professional-business-email) address so you can stand out and look professional.

GoDaddy Invoicing is powered by [GoDaddy Payments, our own secure payment processor](https://www.godaddy.com/payments). With advanced online encryption and the strictest PCI compliance standards, rest assured all transactions — sent and received — are secured.

[Find the best payment processors for small businesses here.](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/best-payment-processors)

### Are there any restrictions on who can use GoDaddy Payments?

Yes, certain categories of businesses and business practices are restricted from using GoDaddy Payments. [Please see here for more information.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/prohibited-businesses-with-godaddy-payments-40682)

### How does Rate Saver work?

Rate Saver is an opt-in feature available on the Point of Sale Plus plan and Invoicing & Pay Links Plus plan. It allows you to save even more on payment processing fees. With Rate Saver, you can add a surcharge to credit card transactions and benefit from lower debit card fees. [Learn more.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/enabling-rate-saver-41781) Rate Saver applies to transactions on the Smart Terminal, Card Reader, Invoicing, Online Pay Links, Virtual Terminal, and the GoDaddy Commerce App. Your customers will be automatically notified of credit card surcharges during checkout and on receipts.

For more information, please refer to our [Surcharging Guide.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/credit-surcharging-guide-for-merchants-41822)
