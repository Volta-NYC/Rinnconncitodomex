Step 5 of the [Set up my Professional Email](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/set-up-professional-email-on-my-phone-and-computer-32292) series.

Add your Professional Email or Professional Email powered by Titan account to the Mail app on your iOS device. Then you can send and receive email from your iPhone or iPad.

01. Open ![settings gear settings menu](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/4pIgfoQQyfoJ5bC3n3nUe2/2253eb9f6ef8846081f27402506e66f1/iPhoneiPadSettings.png)**Settings**.
02. Tap **Mail**, and then **Accounts**. Depending on your iOS version, you might need to tap **Passwords & Accounts**.

    ![In Mail, tap Accounts](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/8bDZKBnr8AERHo6XKUIhv/f925be4d11ea63024f54cf157f2d9a26/o365-mail-settings-accounts-01.png)
03. Tap **Add Account**.

    ![In Mail, tap Accounts](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/6CcTyHjML1J2XpxFSq5sAs/d775a8b6c8e816771c5a2cf77fb4c105/o365-add-account-02.png)
04. Tap **Other**.

    ![Tap Other](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/1ekjylaRGXfJNOsoKZeReW/042904bcd4809b59f1e0813f24b206e4/AddAcctMail27800.png)
05. Tap **Add Mail Account**.

    ![Tap Add Mail Account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/7FOQFGF069YjbvGEjUDfVL/76b24d933e9f5c6b71e25146853849b1/AddMailAcct27800.png)
06. Enter your account details, and then tap **Next**.

    ![Enter account details and tap next](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/7F0PiFOLKBWzsiSawYacGQ/edaa958d94b738e02b6fe7dc1a45d199/NewAcctDetails27800.png)
07. Under **New Account,** make sure **IMAP** is selected.
08. In the **Incoming Mail Server** section, enter:

    - **Host name** as **imap.secureserver.net**
    - Your Professional Email **User Name** and **Password**

10. In the **Outgoing Mail Server** section, enter:

    - **Host name** as **smtpout.secureserver.net**
    - Your Professional Email **User Name** and **Password**

12. Tap **Next**.
13. Tap **Save.** You can come back and update settings here at any point.

    ![save settings iphone/ipad mail](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/1Mb50tqUlPpuxmyUn4siM2/48873ef133b4e5b4c1935de81df52189/SaveSet27800.png)

Your Professional Email or Professional Email powered by Titan is now set up on the Mail app.
