## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I set up Virtual Terminal?

Simply create a GoDaddy account to start using GoDaddyPayments and Virtual Terminal.

### Which credit cards are accepted with Virtual Terminal

Virtual Terminal accepts all major credit and debit cards, including: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover.

### Do I need a website to use Virtual Terminal?

No. You don’t need a website to use Virtual Terminal. All you need is a device with internet connection and a GoDaddy Payments account, which you can create for free.

### Can I take payments over the phone using Virtual Terminal?

Yes. Virtual Terminal makes over-the-phone payments simple. Just open your web browser on your smartphone or computer, and enter your customer’s credit card info into Virtual Terminal, then confirm the charge.

### How quickly can I access my funds using Virtual Terminal?

Money is deposited into your bank account as soon as the next business day. Or for a low fee of 1.75%, you can get paid on the same day, including weekends and holidays. [Compare the best credit and debit card payment processing companies.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-are-my-payout-options-4246)

### What type of businesses should use Virtual Terminal?

Virtual Terminal is suitable for almost any business that takes over the phone or remote payments. For example, it’s great for restaurants that take phone orders, doctor’s offices or for professionals like lawyers and accountants.

### What hardware do I need to use Virtual Terminal?

To use Virtual Terminal, all you need is a computer, mobile phone or tablet with a web browser. No additional hardware is required.

### Is accepting payments with Virtual Terminal safe?

Yes. Virtual Terminal is powered by GoDaddy Payments, [our secure payment processor](https://www.godaddy.com/payments). We follow strict PCI compliance standards and use advanced online encryption to conceal sensitive information and keep your business and customers safe.

### Why should I use GoDaddy’s virtual merchant solution?

With GoDaddy’s virtual merchant solution, Virtual Terminal, you can take over-the-phone or remote payments quickly and securely using any device, like your computer, mobile phone, or tablet.
