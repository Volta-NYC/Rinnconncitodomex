## Professional Business Email FAQ

### What is a professional business email?

Professional Email is when you customize your email address to match your domain name, for example you@yourcompany.com. Professional Email adds much more credibility to your business than yourcompany@gmail.com, as Gmail is not considered specifically for business, and people are 9 times more likely to interact with a professional business email address.

### What are the benefits of having an email address that has a custom email domain?

A domain email address, also known as a professional email address, helps you look more professional with customers and promotes your website and company. In fact, customers are 9 times more likely to choose a company with a professional email address. Every time you send an email from a domain-based address, you're putting your domain name in front of customers and encouraging them to visit your website.

And with each plan, you can create free “alias” addresses — like one using your first name (keith@your-business-name.com) and others for different departments (sales@your-business-name.com) — that all feed into a single inbox and make you look even more professional.

### If I purchase the Business Professional plan, can I download the 5 installs of Office apps on my employees' computers?

You can but we don’t recommend it. The desktop Office app licenses that come with the Business Professional plan are associated with an individual user’s account, including that individual’s sign in and password information. Downloading a copy onto an employee’s computer would give them access to your documents.

We highly recommend that anyone in your organization needing the latest desktop version of Office apps have their own Business Professional account.

### What is email hosting?

Email hosting allows you to set up a professional, custom email address that matches your website’s domain, like you@yourdomain.com. It also helps keep your emails secure and easily accessible, wherever you are.

### How can I be certain that my hosted business email is secure?

Professional Business Email plans protect your email against spam, malware, and known threats with Exchange Online Protection through Microsoft 365. Advanced Email Security is available as an add-on if you’re in need of an extra layer of security.

### Do GoDaddy's email plans include a spam filter?

Yes, GoDaddy's email plans allow you to set up and manage spam filters through Microsoft 365, so your inbox is never overwhelmed by junk mail.

### Does GoDaddy offer free email forwarding?

Email forwarding is a feature included in your M365 Email subscription from GoDaddy. Use email forwarding to create email aliases for specific business purposes (e.g., info@yourcompany.com, sales@ support@). Simply forward those to your main email account and all messages get delivered to a single email box. Or simply forward your domain-based email (you@yourcompany.com) to your personal email (you@gmail.com). You will look professional and appear like a bigger business to your customers.
