## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is CashParking®?

CashParking® is a service that allows you to generate revenue from parked domain names. It works by placing relevant advertisements on parked domains that you have associated with your CashParking® account. You earn money each time a visitor clicks on one of the advertisements.

If you have the Premium CashParking® plan, you receive 80% of click-through revenue. Both CashParking® plans allow you to associate an unlimited number of domains to your CashParking® account.

### Why should I park my domains with GoDaddy?

Some great reasons for parking domains with GoDaddy CashParking® include:

- **Monetization of idle domains:** Start earning money from your unused domains by displaying relevant ads. There's no need to actively develop or maintain a website. It's really as simple as it sounds.
- **Regular payouts:** GoDaddy uses a regular schedule for payouts [(see CashParking® details)](https://www.godaddy.com/help/cashparking-faq-1331), providing an opportunity for CashParking® to serve as a steady stream of income.
- **GoDaddy Auctions membership:** GoDaddy is the leader in domain auctions – running thousands of auctions on a daily basis – and membership to our Auctions platform is included FREE with your CashParking® plan.
- **Low risk:** Our FREE CashParking® membership provides you with a great opportunity to test out our services and see if it’s something that works well for you.

In addition to the above, you get monthly CashParking® reports, standard domain privacy, and access to our award-winning customer support team, who are available to answer any of your CashParking®-related questions.

### If I register a domain elsewhere, can I still park it with GoDaddy?

Yes. If you registered your domain name elsewhere, you can monetize it with us by selecting the CashParking® plan you want to use and entering your domain name. You will need to change your nameservers through your current registrar to point to one of the below CashParking® nameservers:

ns01.cashparking.com

ns02.cashparking.com

### How is revenue generated through CashParking®?

Once you've set up domains in your CashParking® account, GoDaddy begins displaying relevant advertisements on those domains, pulling from our network of advertising partners. Each time visitors to your domain click on one of the displayed ads, the advertising partner pays a certain amount. This click-through revenue is then divided between the service provider (GoDaddy) and the domain owner (you). The Premium plan gives you 80% of this revenue share.

The revenue generated with each click varies based on the displayed advertisement that was clicked. Several different factors - such as the quality of your domain; the amount of traffic it receives; the nature of that traffic; etc. - determine the type of advertisements placed on your page.

### How many domains can I park under my CashParking® account?

An unlimited number of domains can be associated with your CashParking® account. Our easy-to-use platform allows you to:

- Add up to 500 domains at one time.
- Manage all of your parked domains through just one account.
- Upgrade (or downgrade) your CashParking® plan whenever you want.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my Cashparking® plan?

You can upgrade your plan at [https://account.godaddy.com/products?go\_redirect=disabled](https://account.godaddy.com/products?go_redirect=disabled). To downgrade your plan, please cancel your existing membership and sign up for a new plan.

### How do I get paid?

To receive commission for your CashParking® account, you must select one of the following payment methods: direct deposit, PayPal®, Good As Gold, or check.

Electronic payment options

- We make payments midway through the second month following the month they're earned. For example, we pay commission you earned in January in mid-March.
- PayPal® is subject to a USD $25.00 minimum. To ensure that you can receive payments, see PayPal®'s International Payment Restrictions.
- Good As Gold accounts are subject to a USD $5.00 minimum.

Domestic (U.S.) check payments

U.S.-based check payments are subject to a USD $100.00 minimum. A USD $25.00 service fee is applied. If you do not earn the monthly minimum, your total commission rolls over to the following month until your total earnings reach the minimum. Checks are issued on approximately the 15th of each month.

We deduct the USD $25.00 service fee from the total amount paid.

Use one of the following payment methods to avoid service fees and lower your minimum amount:

- Direct Deposit
- PayPal®
- Good As Gold

International check payments

International check payments are subject to a USD $100.00 minimum, and there are no service fees. If you do not earn the minimum in a quarter, the total commission you earned rolls over to the next quarter until your earnings meet the minimum. We send checks to international customers approximately 45 days after the end of each quarter.

Cancellation Notice

If you cancel your account, a USD $15.00 service fee is deducted from the unpaid funds in your account. After all applicable fees, we pay the remaining balance using your selected payment option.
