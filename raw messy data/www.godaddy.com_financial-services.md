## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does GoDaddy Rate Saver work?

Rate Saver is available as an add-on feature for the following plans: Point of Sale Plus, Invoicing, and Pay Links. It provides additional savings on payment processing fees so you can surcharge credit card transactions and benefit from lower debit card fees.

### What products is Rate Saver available for?

Rate Saver can be used on in-person and manually entered credit card transactions on any of the [GoDaddy POS System](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale) Smart Terminal devices; online payments through [Invoicing](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/invoicing) and [Online Pay Links](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/pay-links); payments through [Virtual Terminal](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/virtual-terminal); and manually entered [Tap to Pay](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/tap-to-pay) transactions on the GoDaddy Mobile App.

Rate Saver is enjoyed by numerous merchants in the [retail](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale/retail) space as well as the [food and beverage](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale/restaurants) industry.

### How will my customers be informed of surcharges?

Customers are automatically notified about surcharges during checkout and on their receipts.

### How does GoDaddy Capital work?

- Access your Commerce Home portal to apply in just a few clicks with no paperwork or impact to your credit.
- Review your offers and select the terms that meet your business needs.
- Once approved your funds are available in as little as 24 hours.
- GoDaddy Capital automatically sets aside a portion of your sales, allowing you to pay back as you earn. This means, you’ll pay back more when sales are strong, and less when sales are slow.
- After your initial cash advance you can apply for additional funds as needed. Learn more about [merchant cash advance](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/merchant-cash-advance) with GoDaddy.

### Am I eligible for GoDaddy Capital?

Merchants who use GoDaddy payment processing are invited to apply. When you log in to your Commerce Home dashboard, a message will also appear inviting you to view your available funding offers. Eligibility and credit decisions are determined by our partners, YouLend and Pipe Technologies Inc., which will consider your business’s processing history, account history, payment frequency, and other business attributes. Learn more and get started with GoDaddy Payments.

### What fees are associated with GoDaddy Capital?

There is a flat fee to access GoDaddy Capital, but no interest fees or prepayment penalties. You will pay a fixed percentage based on the loan amount.

### What payout speed options are available, and how quickly will I receive my funds with each option?

You can choose from standard (1–2 business days), same-day, or instant payouts. With same-day payouts, funds are typically deposited within one hour of your scheduled close of day, or 6PM PT for most close of day times, or by 1AM PT if your close of day is 11:59PM PT. Complete an instant bank transfer to access your eligible balance on-demand, 24/7, and you’ll usually receive funds within 20 minutes of the request.
