01. Open Samsung Email.


    ![White envelope on red background with Email written underneath](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/6GvKb2A4F6Hb8Auiqd6zjU/a90bd9dcae3e05edb18d0b1e87982503/reuse-Samsung-Email-App-072720.png)
02. If you're a new user, skip to step 3. Otherwise, tap ![Three horizontal lines stacked on each other](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/7l0Hvkp1BECnTT4CdCGYr/42e385216bca9144cc0f6d66c6b2b47b/reuse-Samsung-Email-Menu-072720.png)**Menu**, ![settings gear settings menu](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/297NeIr0LpOOqHOHlyQRTf/5c14519322ea04b08faac01137c18ec8/image-article-16561-03.png)**Settings**, and then **Add account**.
03. Tap **Office365**.


    ![Screen with different email client options including Office365 with red square](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/KkSrxa5RlFDL0CE0G0doH/f2b4f1a40f4be11402dccc515a3a8206/m365-set-up-email-office-365-01.png)
04. Enter your Microsoft 365 email address, and then tap **Next**.

    **Note:** If Microsoft can't find your account, you'll be asked to enter your details manually:


    - **Username**: Your Microsoft 365 email address
    - **Password**: Your Microsoft 365 email password
    - **Server**: outlook.office365.com
    - **Domain\\username**: \\\[Microsoft 365 email address\] (example: _\\jane@coolexample.com_)

05. If you see a prompt that says your email is used with more than one account with Microsoft, tap **Work or school** account (not **Personal** account).
06. Enter your email password, and then tap **Sign In**.


    ![The M365 sign-in with an example email address and password entered.](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/2wMbvLsTPgYPrsUcwm9CRS/515ba7c59fe858ec9731b407d534e883/image-m365-sso.png)
07. If you're signing in from a new device or location, you'll see a prompt to **Verify it's you** and confirm your identity through a one-time code.


    ![The Verify it's you with the three options for a one-time code.](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/6t27Uie4bQ05hnqxgWAgEO/f44d21e85e854b02b649220afd3a9ac8/image-m365-verify-you-prompt.png)
08. If you're asked to trust your domain, tap **Continue**.


    ![The prompt asking if you trust your domain from Microsoft.](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/5XxlWpuSSpKNNUxnUlD4SB/6287101c413555de15179050b6b4b522/image-m365-trust-domain.png)
09. If it's your first time signing in and multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enabled, you'll see a prompt to set up your authentication method. (If you have trouble following the on-screen instructions, [trying setting up MFA using these steps instead](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/set-up-my-multi-factor-authentication-method-40415).)
10. If you already have MFA set up, verify your identity using your authentication method.
11. You might get a request for permission to access your account, tap **Accept**.
12. If you're asked to apply security settings, tap **Apply**.
13. Under **Manual setup**, tap **Done**. You can come back and edit these settings at any point.

    ![Manual setup and Done button](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/36qrdHafiZltUPFrwf2HBx/5f72586c5870d4f7d7bb10e78d294b13/image-article-16561-manualsetup.png)
14. If you're asked to activate security features on your device, tap **Activate**.



    ![Activate, uninstall or cancel security features on your device](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3qug2LaRiA9wcZkdODsXsH/5e42d4ab123c079d6e4ec9196117bfc3/image-article-16561-activate.png)
15. (Optional) Edit your **Account name**. This name is visible to recipients when you send email, so you might want to use your business name here.

    ![Enter name and tap and Done](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/4gheUtFfS799g0BX9oF6Yi/68f62587d1131dd7e38a70dd2749cde0/image-article-16561-accountname.png)
16. Tap **Done**.

Your Microsoft 365 is now set up on your Samsung Email app and you can see your inbox.
