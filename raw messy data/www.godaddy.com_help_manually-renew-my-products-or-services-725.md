# Manually renew my products or services

Manually renew your products at any time, even if they're set to auto-renew.

**Note:** If you're looking to renew an _expired domain_, [the steps are different](https://www.godaddy.com/help/renew-my-expired-domains-5018).

1. Go to your [Renewals and Billing](https://account.godaddy.com/myrenewals) page and select **Manage Subscriptions**.
2. In the **Subscriptions** list, select the product and then select **Renew Now**.

![select renew now](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/4jTEL2KHGiarpAtEZhTLr0/b7313af78bbad0118d4279de1751e793/mya-select-renew-now.png)


We'll take you to the Cart, where you can see the renewal price.
3. Follow the prompts to complete your renewal purchase.

**Note:** Products [auto-renew](https://www.godaddy.com/help/turn-on-auto-renew-20007) for the same length of time as their most recent manual renewal, instead of their original registration length. This also applies to domains that are manually renewed after July 16, 2020. For example, if a domain is manually renewed after July 16, 2020 for a three-year term, it will auto-renew for the same three-year term when the time comes. For full details, see our [Domain Name Registration Agreement](https://www.godaddy.com/legal/agreements/domain-name-registration-agreement).
