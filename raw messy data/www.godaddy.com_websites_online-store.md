## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an ecommerce website?

An ecommerce website is an online platform for buying and selling products, services, and content. It features product galleries, shopping carts, checkout systems, and backend functions for processing payments, shipping, customer support, and marketing. Essentially, it acts as a digital storefront that mirrors the experience of a physical store.

### How to build an online store?

An online store gives your business a unique edge that no brick-and-mortar ever could — the ability to serve customers from around the world, 24/7/365. If you’re ready to open your doors online, here’s how to get up and selling in no time.

1. **Pick your products:** Think about what you want to sell online, and make sure you have the resources or inventory to list items — be it services or traditional products — for sale on your site.
2. **Choose a plan:** If you’re offering services, like yoga lessons or career coaching, check out our Premium plan. It lets you accept payments from customers who book services on your website. If you need to sell products, opt for the Ecommerce plan to get everything you need to power your online store.
3. **Set up your online store:** Our easy-to-customize store templates help you quickly set up your store, complete with product descriptions, prices, shipping and payment options. Just pick one to get started, or dive right into the store builder and type in your industry to start with a relevant template.
4. **Pick a domain name:** Your domain name is the address where your online store lives. Pick something that’s the same as, or similar to, your business name. This makes it easier for people to remember your website’s address and reinforces your brand.
5. **Start selling online:** This is the fun part! Sell your products and accept payments directly on your online store, or connect your site to popular marketplaces and social channels, like Amazon or Instagram, to sell in more places. Don’t worry, your inventory syncs across all your sales channels, so you can avoid overselling while tracking everything from your GoDaddy Online Store dashboard.
6. **Market your business:** Let people know about your business and what services or products you offer with built-in marketing tools. Create email campaigns, reply to social posts and track your online presence right from your GoDaddy Online Store dashboard. The more you show up online in all the places your customers shop, the more chances you get to turn online interactions into customers.

### How do I sell products or services in my web shop?

You only need to [add one product](https://www.godaddy.com/help/-23906) or service before publishing your site for the first time, and generally it’s better to get your site online as soon as possible. If you’re having a tough time getting started, or you need the extra push to get your goods online, you won’t have to worry about complicated website tech — GoDaddy Online Store is easy to use. After you’ve published once you can update your payment and shipping methods. The default setting will be [GoDaddy Payments](https://www.godaddy.com/payments) — how your customers pay for your products or services, and the [default shipping method](https://www.godaddy.com/help/-23917) will be free shipping, which you’ll want to change if you are selling anything other than services, and need your customers to compensate you for shipping costs. We’ve also added Apple Pay, so customers with Apple Pay enabled devices will be able to benefit from a quick click-through checkout process.

You absolutely can, and [you should.](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/how-to-promote-your-blog-on-social-media) You’ll want to build a strong online presence so you can reach your customers wherever they are, and get them to go to your online store. You can link to Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Google and YouTube. Connecting all your social media tools gives your customers a memorable experience. They can follow you however they like, comment on your store and products, and easily find out where they can purchase your awesome products. ### How do I ship products to my customers, and what are my options for shipping methods?

You have several flexible options. With GoDaddy Online Store you can ship via Canada Post and United Parcel Service (UPS); both of which ship domestic and international and are the most accurate shipping methods based on location. You can also ship using a flat-rate per order, which is sometimes easier for everyone, or pick weight-based shipping — which charges different amounts based on how much an order weighs. The option is up to you on [how you want to ship your products](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/top-notch-shipping-tips-for-your-online-store), but we’ve provided several options to make your ecommerce website what [you want it to be](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/online-store-shipping-strategies-maximize-sales).
