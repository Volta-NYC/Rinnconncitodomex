## POS Systems FAQ

### What is a POS (Point of Sale) system?

Point of sale (POS) is where a sale happens, where a customer pays for goods or services. POS transactions can occur in person, over the phone or online. POS hardware is most often used to conduct in-person sales. A POS terminal or machine is typically a hardware device used at businesses to accept payments from customers through cards (credit, debit or prepaid) and digital wallet (Apple Pay or Google Pay).

### What is a POS terminal or POS machine?

POS is an acronym for Point of Sale. A POS terminal or machine is typically a hardware device used at businesses to accept payments from customers through cards (credit, debit, or prepaid) and digital wallets (Apple Pay or Google Pay).

### How does a POS system work?

POS systems usually consist of hardware and software. The POS hardware is the physical device that accepts the payment. The POS software is what processes the payment and enables the business to complete the sales transaction.

### How long does it take for funds to appear in my bank account?

For transactions that are processed on a business day before 5 PM Pacific Time, the funds will appear the next business day. If the transactions are processed on a business day after 5 PM PT, the funds will appear in 2+ days. Transactions processed on a Friday will not appear until after the weekend.

### How does Rate Saver work?

Rate Saver is an opt-in feature available on the Point of Sale Plus plan and Invoicing & Pay Links Plus plan. It allows you to save even more on payment processing fees. With Rate Saver, you can add a surcharge to credit card transactions and benefit from lower debit card fees. [Learn More](https://www.godaddy.com/help/enabling-rate-saver-41781). Rate Saver applies to transactions on the Smart Terminal, Card Reader, Invoicing, Online Pay Links, Virtual Terminal, and the GoDaddy Commerce App. Your customers will be automatically notified of credit card surcharges during checkout and on receipts.

For more information, please refer to our [Surcharging Guide](https://www.godaddy.com/help/credit-surcharging-guide-for-merchants-41822).
