# Turn on auto-renew

Follow these steps to turn on auto-renew for your products. Your products will automatically renew on their expiration dates.

**Note:** For products set to auto-renew: If the selected payment method can't be charged at billing time, we may try another payment method that has been successfully used before.

1. Go to your [Renewals and Billing](https://account.godaddy.com/myrenewals) page and select **Manage Subscriptions**.
2. Select the product and then select the **Auto-Renew** toggle.


![select auto renew toggle](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3WYhSjoo6UjUOVVMmrEz8X/cf8950b5139b8ee12459a629c6bc4cbe/mya-select-auto-renew.png)


The Subscriptions list now shows the date that your product will auto-renew.

**Note:** If auto-renew is already turned on for your product, you will not see the **Auto-Renew** box or toggle.


### Change renewal length and see new price

Follow these steps to see the price for your product before turning on auto-renew.

1. Go to your [Renewals and Billing](https://account.godaddy.com/myrenewals) page and select **Manage Subscriptions**.
2. Select the product and then select **Edit Plan**. We'll show you your current plan and its price, as well as other available plans.
