## GoDaddy Online Pay Links FAQ

### What are Pay Links?

A Pay Link or payment link is a clickable URL or QR code. It’s a quick and easy way to take online payments — no website needed. Just create a Pay Link, share it with your customers and they’ll complete their purchase from a secure checkout page.

### Are Online Pay Links safe?

Yes. Pay Links is powered by GoDaddy Payments, [our own secure payment processor](https://www.godaddy.com/payments). We follow strict PCI compliance standards and use advanced encryption to hide sensitive information, keeping your business and customer data safe.

### How can I create a free online Payment Link?

Create a free payment link simply by signing up for a GoDaddy Payments account, where you’ll be able to set up a pay link in minutes. With the Plus plan ($9.99/mo with annual plan or $11.99/mo if you pay month-to-month) you get additional features, such as:

- Discounted rate: 2.8% + 30¢ per Pay Link card transaction, and 0.8% with a $10 maximum per ACH transaction
- Options to collect billing and shipping addresses
- Branded receipt emails
- Send receipts from your business email address
- Set up employee roles and permissions for more control

### Do I need a website to send a Pay Link?

No. You don’t need a website to send a payment link. GoDaddy Pay Links automatically creates a secure checkout page for each of your pay links, so you don’t need an ecommerce website. If you have a website made with GoDaddy, you can add your Pay Links as pay buttons on your site.

### How quickly can I access my funds?

Money is deposited into your bank account by the next business day. Or for a low fee of 1.75%, you can get paid on the same day, including weekends and holidays. [Learn more about payout options.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-are-my-payout-options-42464)

### What types of businesses and organizations should use Pay Links?

Pay Links works for all types of businesses. With Pay Links, you can accept payments for products, services, donations, and beyond.

### Can I customize a Pay Link with my brand’s logo?

Yes. You can customize a Pay Link with your brand’s logo, business name, image, description, and link address to look even more professional. With the paid plan, Plus, send branded receipt emails from your business email. [Learn more about the Plus plan.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/understand-the-invoicing-and-pay-links-plus-plan-42276)

### Can I use Pay Links to generate a QR code?

Yes. You can use Pay Links to create a QR code that customers can scan using their mobile phone for contactless payment. Once you’ve created a pay link, you’ll have the option to transform it into a QR code with just one click.

### Can I send an invoice using Pay Links?

You cannot send an invoice using Pay Links, but you can use the Pay Link to take payments on an invoice that you’ve already issued to a customer. Learn more about [business invoicing](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/invoicing) from GoDaddy.

### How can I add Pay Links to my existing website?

Once you’ve created a Pay Link, you can copy and paste it to your website and accept payments for a product or service.

### Are there any restrictions on who can use GoDaddy Payments?

Yes, there are categories of businesses and business practices that are restricted from using GoDaddy Payments. [Please see here for more information.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/prohibited-businesses-with-godaddy-payments-40682)

### Why choose GoDaddy for Pay Links?

GoDaddy payment links are the simplest way to take payments and have the lowest fees in the industry. Pay Links are included with GoDaddy Payments, a secure payment processor. [Find the best payment processors for small businesses here.](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/best-payment-processors)

### Can I use the commerce app to send and receive Pay Links?

Yes you can download the GoDaddy Commerce App to create and send Pay Links. You can also easily view and edit the links you create in the app on your web dashboard. [Learn more about using Pay Links on the Commerce App.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/using-pay-links-in-the-commerce-app-42226)

### What payment methods can a customer use on a Pay Link?

Your customers can pay using all major credit and debit cards, as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay. You can also provide customers with another simple, secure way to pay directly through a bank transfer with ACH.

### How long does it take to process an ACH payment for Pay Links?

It takes between 3-5 business days for you to receive your ACH payment. Learn more about ACH payments [here.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-ach-bank-transfer-42565)
