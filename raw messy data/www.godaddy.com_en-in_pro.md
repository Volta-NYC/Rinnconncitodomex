## GoDaddy Pro FAQ

### Why should web designers and developers choose GoDaddy?

GoDaddy provides a full suite of hosting, domain, and business tools tailored for web professionals. Our platform offers flexibility, scalability, and a streamlined method to manage clients, sites, and projects all in one location

### What is the Hub by GoDaddy Pro?

The Hub is a free dashboard designed for web professionals to simplify site maintenance, manage multiple clients, and automate routine tasks, allowing you to dedicate more time to growing your business.

### Which hosting solution is right for me?

- [Managed Hosting for Wordpress](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/hosting/web-hosting) is Ideal for designers who want an optimized, hassle-free WordPress experience.
- [VPS Hosting](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/hosting/vps-hosting) is best for developers who need full control and performance for complex projects.
- [Web Hosting](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/hosting/web-hosting) is great for developers looking for a cost-effective, flexible hosting environment.
- [Website Builder](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/websites/website-builder) is perfect for designers who want a fast, no-code website with built-in marketing tools.

### How does the GoDaddy Reseller program work?

The Reseller Program allows you to sell GoDaddy products under your own brand, set your own pricing, and earn recurring revenue. Additionally, it integrates with Hub by GoDaddy Pro for convenient store management.

### Can I manage multiple clients with the Hub by GoDaddy Pro?

Yes, the Hub by GoDaddy Pro enables you to manage multiple sites, facilitate updates, and even automate maintenance tasks for your entire client base.

### What are the benefits of the GoDaddy Agency Program?

The agency program connects you with potential clients, offers premium WordPress support, and enables you to earn commissions on referred GoDaddy products, all while providing you with tools to scale your business. We’ll assign you an agency success manager, serving as your single point of contact.

### Can I hire designers and developers to help with my project workload?

Absolutely. With our [Web Design Services](https://www.godaddy.com/), you can engage GoDaddy experts to assist with client projects, enabling you to pursue more business without hiring full-time staff.
