## .AI Domain FAQ

### What is the .ai domain?

The .ai domain represents artificial intelligence, arguably one of humanity’s most important innovations. Technically, the .ai domain name was created as a country code domain for Anguilla, a small (only 35 square miles!) British territory in the Eastern Caribbean, but the world recognizes those two little letters as something far bigger.

Artificial intelligence is already here. It’s beating the world’s best chess players, recommending products for us on our favorite online stores and even giving us directions from our smartphones — that’s right, you’re carrying artificial intelligence with you every day in your pocket or purse. But it’s about to become a lot bigger and smarter, so it’s time we give it its own domain.

### Why get a .ai domain name?

If you work in the field of artificial intelligence, there are two things you need to do: Give careful consideration to instrumental convergence and register a **.ai** domain name. We won’t get into the first one — frankly, that’s above our pay grade — but we’re experts on that second one.

Here are some key points to consider:

- .ai domains are highly relevant for businesses in the artificial intelligence and technology industries.
- Having a .ai domain can help establish credibility and expertise in the field of AI.
- .ai domains are unique and memorable, making it easier for customers to remember and find your website.
- .ai domain extensions are short and concise, making them easier to type and share.
- .ai domains are still relatively new and not as saturated as other domain extensions, allowing for more availability and options for choosing a desired domain name.

A specialized field deserves a specialized domain name. You’re not just some company or network — you’re working on (arguably) the greatest advancement of the modern world. A generic domain extension just looks plain and uninspired. But a **.ai** domain name looks advanced and futuristic, just like the work you’re doing. And for potential visitors who don’t know what you do, your domain name will tell them before they even reach your site.

### Who is .ai for?

.ai domains are perfect for small business owners involved in various applications of artificial intelligence, including machine learning, robotics, deep learning, computer vision and more. .Ai is also a country code, and you might think that Anguilla would require you to set up some sort of presence in order to register a **.ai**, but that’s not the case. They’ve made sure that anyone can register a **.ai** domain name — which has its pros and cons.

While that means companies that work in the field of artificial intelligence are free to register their **.ai** domain without restrictions, no matter where they’re located, it also means that people who don’t work in artificial intelligence could potentially snag your name before you do.

Don't hesitate to register your **.ai** domain today to avoid losing it to competitors or random individuals.

### Is .ai a top-level domain (TLD)?

Yes. More specifically, it’s a country-code top-level domain (ccTLD) that was originally created for Anguilla. In practice, it has become a global favorite for companies and creators working in the field of artificial intelligence. So, while it started as a geographic domain, the world largely sees “.ai” as a clear signal: _this domain is about AI._

### Are .ai domains trusted?

Absolutely. The .ai domain name is a trusted, legitimate extension used by startups, researchers, developers, and well-known brands across the AI space. Just like any other TLD, trust ultimately comes down to what you do with it. By securing your domain name on a reliable platform and launching a professional website, you build a credible online presence for your venture. The .ai extension communicates what you’re about – the rest is up to your brand.

### Why are .ai domains more expensive?

Think of a .ai domain as prime real estate in the world’s most exclusive tech district. The registry that manages .ai (the government of Anguilla) sets a higher wholesale price due to the specialized nature of the TLD and its massive global demand. While the price for .ai might be higher than a standard .com, most innovators find the AI-first identity to be well worth the investment.

### Who can register a .ai domain?

Anyone can register a .ai domain, no matter their location. Unlike some country-code domains that require a local presence, .ai is widely available to individuals and businesses around the world – whether you’re building an AI product, publishing research, launching a newsletter, or creating something completely new. This unrestricted registration policy is great for accessibility, but it also means there’s been a bit of a digital land grab. If you already have a name you love, it’s best to register it before a competitor (or random individual with a great idea) beats you to it.

### Do .ai domains include free WHOIS privacy?

Yes. When you register a .ai domain with GoDaddy, we include basic WHOIS 100% for free. This keeps your name, email, and phone number hidden from the public database, helping to protect you from spammers and unsolicited offers, so you can focus on building your next big idea.

### Are .ai domains considered premium domains?

Some .ai domains are premium, but not all are. While .ai is considered a high-value extension because of its niche focus, not every .ai domain earns the label of “premium” domain. A premium domain usually refers to a short, generic, or highly sought-after word or phrase, regardless of the extension (think single words or common terms like _cars.com_ or _data.ai_). These domains carry a higher price tag due to their secondary market value. However, most available .ai domain names can be registered at standard, competitive rates. The good news: GoDaddy will show you upfront pricing when you search, so you can find a name fits your brand and budget.
