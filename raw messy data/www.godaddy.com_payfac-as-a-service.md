## Payfac as a Service FAQ

### What is Payfac as a Service?

Payfac as a Service, or Payment Facilitation, allows companies that offer business management software to embed payments into your software platform with a ready-to-integrate payment technology platform. With a single integration, you can offer an omnichannel payment experience within your platform, so small businesses have an integrated solution that includes payment options.

### What are the benefits of PayFac as a Service?

Payfac as a Service enables you to offer a seamless payment experience for your customers without the overhead or liability that comes with PCI compliance, risk management, security/encryption, and more. The advanced and reliable platform is ready to integrate so you can go to market faster and offer a best-in-class experience to your customers so they can accept payments in person, online, or both, have the flexibility to accept modern payment options; Debit, credit, cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and HSA/FSA.

### Why choose GoDaddy as a partner?

GoDaddy’s payment platform is built on advanced technology that can drive immediate value to your business and to your customers. We manage the liability of PCI compliance, risk management, and security/encryption in the ready-to-integrate payment platform and offer a managed service to integrate payment technology into your business software. With GoDaddy, you can drive additional revenue opportunities beyond payments with a limitless, all-in-one omnicommerce platform to run all aspects of a merchant business. Only GoDaddy integrates the Online Store, major marketplaces, social commerce, Google Shopping, In-person POS, and mPOS without the need for any additional plugins. We also have in-house expertise in hardware, software, and payments and offer a range of hardware choices that can fit any small business needs.

As part of the Partner Program, GoDaddy offers you robust APIs and tooling for implementation, marketing, onboarding, and troubleshooting so you can customize and scale your offering.
