# What shows in the GoDaddy WHOIS directory?

[Our public GoDaddy WHOIS directory](https://godaddy.com/whois) shows specific information for registered domains, including contact, ownership, and registration details. Eligible domains have [Domain Privacy from GoDaddy](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-domain-privacy-41145) automatically applied to hide Registrant contact info. This info will be hidden or substituted with details from our Domain Privacy partner, Domains By Proxy®, when Domain Privacy is set to **On** or **Limited**.

**Note:** Effective August 21, 2025, only Registrant contact details are required and publicly displayed for most domains, depending on registry and [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/consensus-policies/registration-data-policy#registration-data-policy) requirements. Other contact information may be collected but won't be shown in WHOIS records.

### Domain Information

Details about the specific domain, unrelated to any Registrant details.

- **Name**: The domain name
- **Registry Domain ID**: The ID of the domain at the [registry](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-the-difference-between-a-registry-registrar-and-registrant-8039).
- **Registered On**: The date the domain was originally registered
- **Expires On**: The current expiration date for the domain
- **Updated On**: The date the domain was last updated by the Registry (does not apply to Registrant updates)
- **Domain Status**: Current status of the domain from the Registry (does not apply to registrar updates)
- **Nameservers**: Current nameservers assigned to the domain

### Registrant Contact

Contact information for the domain Registrant. The information shown will vary depending on [the current Domain Privacy level](https://www.godaddy.com/help/change-my-domain-privacy-level-32283) of the domain.

- **Registrar Data**: The [registrar](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-the-difference-between-a-registry-registrar-and-registrant-8039) where the domain is registered
- **Name**


  - **Domain Privacy On**: Registration Private
  - **Domain Privacy Limited**: \- (no information displayed)
  - **Domain Privacy Off**: Registrant name listed on the domain
- **Organization**


  - **Domain Privacy On**: Domains By Proxy, LLC
  - **Domain Privacy Limited**: \- (no information displayed)
  - **Domain Privacy Off**: Registrant organization listed on the domain

**Note:** If a Registrant Organization is provided, it becomes the Registered Name Holder [per ICANN policy](https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/consensus-policies/registration-data-policy#registration-data-policy). The Registrant Organization is not shown on RDDS/WHOIS by default, but visibility can be toggled when [adjusting Domain Privacy settings to **Limited** or **Off**](https://www.godaddy.com/help/change-my-domain-privacy-level-32283).
- **Phone**


  - **Domain Privacy On**: +1.4806242599
  - **Domain Privacy Limited**: \- (no information displayed)
  - **Domain Privacy Off**: Registrant phone number listed on the domain
- **Fax**


  - **Domain Privacy On**: \- (no information displayed)
  - **Domain Privacy Limited**: \- (no information displayed)
  - **Domain Privacy Off**: Registrant fax number listed on the domain
- **Email**


  - **Domain Privacy On**: Anonymous contact form link to message the Registrant
  - **Domain Privacy Limited**: Anonymous contact form link to message the Registrant
  - **Domain Privacy Off**: Registrant email address listed on the domain


    - When you choose a [randomized email address](https://www.godaddy.com/help/edit-my-email-privacy-settings-3450), the email address listed here will differ for every domain.
- **Mailing Address**


  - **Domain Privacy On**: DomainsByProxy.com 100 S. Mill Ave, Suite 1600, Tempe, Arizona, 85281
  - **Domain Privacy Limited**: State and Country listed on the domain
  - **Domain Privacy Off**: Full mailing address listed on the domain

## More info

- [Some domains aren't eligible](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-domain-privacy-41145) for Domain Privacy due to registry restrictions. Review the [Domain Name Registration Agreement](https://godaddy.com/legal/agreements/refund-policy) for details.
- If you've decided to invoke [Nominet's WHOIS opt-out policy](https://www.nominet.uk/changes-to-uk-whois-policy/), the visibility of your contact info will be determined by that policy, regardless of your Domain Privacy or [Domain Protection](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-domain-protection-32311) through GoDaddy.
- WHOIS contact details will not be made available via automated bulk lookup points over Port 43.
- GoDaddy has no control over what information a third-party WHOIS directory displays.
