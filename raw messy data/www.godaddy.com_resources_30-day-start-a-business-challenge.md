### Day 3: Define your brand

We’re going to work towards defining the soul of your business- your brand. To do that, we're going to dive into the adjectives that you feel best represent your brand. Are you cool, edgy, elegant, trustworthy, or maybe playful? Start by jotting down a list of adjectives that resonate with you.

Then, let's move on to [brand archetypes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dqPYtnA9Gw). These are the personalities your brand can take on. Think of it as casting the leading role for the story of your business.

Are you friendly and casual, or authoritative and formal? Choose a personality that makes sense for your brand and stick with it.

Here are the 12 brand archetypes:

01. The Creator
02. The Sage
03. The Caregiver
04. The Innocent
05. The Jester
06. The Magician
07. The Ruler
08. The Hero
09. The Regular Guy/Gal
10. The Rebel
11. The Explorer
12. The Lover

To learn more about these archetypes, [download the complete brand archetype deck](https://img1.wsimg.com/cdnassets/m/1f92a69c3b4e9bbd/original/Brand-Archetype-Cards.pdf).

Select your top 5, then narrow it down to your top 3, and finally, your top 1. Don't stress, this isn't set in stone! It's all part of your business journey and can evolve as you grow.

**Task list:**

- Using the steps above, determine your ideal brand archetype.
- Read through the blog posts given to help narrow down your unique brand identity.
