### What is the .io domain?

The **.io** domain extension is mainly used as an abbreviation for Input/Output, which makes it very popular for tech startups in general. The **.io** tld also represents the country code for the British Indian Ocean Territory.

### Top reasons to register a .io domain extension.

Finding a domain name that fits your business is easier than ever with a **.io** TLD. Below are just a few of the reasons why so many people choose to buy a **.io** domain:

**Establish a connection.** For startups and tech-related companies, using a **.io** extension works as a branding move and serves to create a connection with target audiences, who will pick up on the nod to IO in computer science.

**Create fun domain hacks.** The popularity of **.io** websites doesn't stop with tech-related companies. You can use a **.io** extension to tap into your creativity and come up with memorable domain hacks, like cheer.IO, card.IO, portfol.IO, and more.

To sum it up - a **.io** TLD helps you keep things short, sweet and memorable.
