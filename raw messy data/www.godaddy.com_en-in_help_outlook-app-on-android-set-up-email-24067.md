# Outlook app on Android: Set up email

This article is specific to **Business Email**. If you have a different type of email, please see [Microsoft 365 Android instructions](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/add-my-microsoft-365-email-to-outlook-on-android-8905), or [Workspace email Android instructions](https://www.godaddy.com/article/19297).

To access your Business Email from your Android phone, you can use the Outlook app. The Outlook app is compatible with Android phones and tablets running Android 4.0 and above.

**Note:** We've used a Samsung Galaxy 5, for the instructions below.

1. [Download](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.office.outlook&hl=en), install, and open the Outlook app.
2. If you haven't already set up an account, tap **Get started**. If you have already set up an account:


1. Tap the Settings gear in the top right corner of your inbox.

      ![Tap settings cog in inbox](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/4dRTsji7YWdryMuo2OIhzb/7d3bd5a9a6977c0971a4be53d094da4e/business-email-outlook-app-android-set-up-email-01.png)
2. Tap **Add Account**.

      ![Tap Add account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/5BsuVHwBMTXCON7v3nE48N/2480fd1c686f6e6d738efb69c557ff3e/business-email-tap-add-account-02.png)
3. Tap **Add an email account**.

      ![Tap Add an email account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/2jA7YPgJmjekAJdR9Qen46/fe2e0c89bf41cd4d720d90e122f17626/business-email-tap-add-email-account-03.png)
3. Enter your Business Email address, and tap **Continue**.

![Enter email address, tap Continue](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3hV2x08kbV1vX05YrQhP5s/a2227578e4ba5156bd24aa648272da69/business-email-enter-email-address-tap-continue-04.png)
4. The Outlook app may have you select the type of email. Tap **IMAP**.

![Tap IMAP, if prompted](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/6a1wg8nMiUl8vV0RuxJJS7/b64d25eb62d5513ed7b5203d174fd36e/business-email-tap-IMAP-05.png)
5. Your **Email Address** should be pre-filled. Tap **Show advanced settings**.

![Tap Show advanced settings](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3Tgbi01VAyaY7BAzWIKdhp/d88e6bae3834f04c5a511a87bb424549/business-email-tap-show-advanced-settings-06.png)
6. Enter the following details:




| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Display Name | The name you want to show to others when they receive your emails. |
| Description | An optional reference for you about this account. No one else will see this. |
| IMAP Host Name | The [correct incoming server address for your region, found here.](https://www.godaddy.com/article/24071) |
| IMAP Username | Your full Business Email password. |
| IMAP Password | Your Business Email password. |
| SMTP Host Name | The [correct outgoing server address for your region, found here.](https://www.godaddy.com/article/24071) |
| SMTP Username | Your full Business Email password, again. |
| SMTP Password | Your Business Email password, again. |

![Enter email details](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/5S055SSAocuVefwhrSpgEb/dc8ce0d9b5afdfb4b5d12905e60875b4/business-email-enter-email-account-details-tap-checkmark-07.png)
7. Tap the check mark in the top right corner of the screen.
8. If you want to set up another email address, tap **Let's do it!** Otherwise, tap **Maybe later** to get to your inbox.

![Choose to add another account, or not](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/6IPjX93GGaYetAg9tbaXUh/32c72e2759803368faeb9583a8bf62f4/business-email-add-another-account-08.png)
