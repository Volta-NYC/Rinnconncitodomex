### What are .uk domain names?

The official domain of the United Kingdom, **dot** **uk** is short, sharp and a clean slate for digital natives and pioneers looking for a fresh take on the web.

When the **.uk** **domain name** first hit the market, many owners of a related third-level domain (like .co **.uk** or .me **.uk**) had first right of refusal on the matching **dot** **uk**. Five years later, any reserved **.uk** **domain names** that hadn’t been claimed were released for open registration.
