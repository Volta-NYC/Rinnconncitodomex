## Retail POS System and Software FAQ

### What is a retail Point of Sale (POS) system?

[POS hardware](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale/hardware) can include cash registers, barcode scanners, receipt printers, and computers or tablets.

[POS Software](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale/software) typically includes options to process sales and payments, manage inventory, record transactions, gather data and analytics, employee management, and integration with other business tools.

### Why is it important to get the right Point of Sale for my retail business?

The right POS system will help your business to speed up checkouts and reduce human error when ringing up transactions. It will track inventory levels and manage stock. It can also help facilitate personalized customer experiences using targeted promotions and loyalty programs. It provides valuable insights into business operations through detailed sales reports and data analysis.

### What are the benefits of a retail POS system?

A retail Point of Sale system offers a quick and easy setup for business owners, allowing their employees to quickly get up-to-speed. It helps to speed up the checkout process, manage inventory, and allows businesses to accept most forms of payment. You can collect data on sales, promotions, inventory and more to make decision-making easier.

### How do I set up and install the POS system and software in my retail store?

Order your [GoDaddy Smart Terminal](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale), and once it arrives charge it fully and complete the setup process.

1\. Explore the interface: Familiarize yourself with the pre-loaded apps on the Smart Terminal, and the new areas in your GoDaddy Payments online dashboard.

2\. Create your product catalog: Add your products to your dashboard by importing them from your existing store, or manually enter them and set up taxes, discounts, and fees as needed.

3\. Process test transactions: Complete a few test transactions to get comfortable with the order process. Practice creating orders, processing payments, and issuing refunds.

4\. Customize your terminal: Personalize your Smart Terminal by adding your company logo, creating user accounts, connecting accessories (like cash drawers, or printers) and exploring additional apps in the App Center.

5\. Consider Point of Sale Plus plan: Evaluate upgrading to the Point of Sale Plus plan for additional features like lower transaction fees, unlimited products, stock counts and alerts, and the ability to set up user roles and permissions to manage employee access on your online dashboard.

GoDaddy offers extensive support resources including troubleshooting guides, video tutorials, and direct 24/7 customer support.

### What are the costs involved with purchasing and maintaining the retail POS system and software?

The hardware for our GoDaddy Smart Terminal may cost between $0-$399.00, depending on which device is right for your business. Built-in software means that you are ready to go right out of the box. The processing fees from [GoDaddy Payments](https://www.godaddy.com/payments) for in-person payments are 2.5% + 0¢ or as low as 2.3% + 0¢ when you add the Point of Sale Plus plan. Another advantage of upgrading to the Point of Sale Plus plan for $28.99 per month (when paid annually) is the ability to access advanced POS software features like unlimited products, inventory stock alerts, and the ability to set up customized user roles and permissions.

No, a GoDaddy Smart Terminal is required to take advantage of the competitive GoDaddy Payments rates and access our POS features that help streamline operations and ensure a smooth checkout experience.

### Is GoDaddy POS compatible with Website Builder?

If you plan to take your retail business online, you can effortlessly create a website, and online store using [GoDaddy's AI Website Builder](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/ai-website-builder). Drop an image into the tool to instantly create a shoppable item in your online catalog.

### What payment methods are supported by GoDaddy retail POS?

GoDaddy's retail POS system supports a wide range of payment methods to give your customers plenty of options: Major credit and debit cards (including EMV chip cards), contactless payments, and digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as HSA/FSA cards.

You also get additional payment options with GoDaddy Payments. You can send invoices, create Pay Links for easy online payments, and accept [Tap-to-Pay payments](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/tap-to-pay) on your Android or iPhone using the GoDaddy mobile app. These options are useful if you also offer services, sell via your social channels, or take payments away from your store.
