# Android: Set up email

This article is specific to **Business Email**. If you have a different type of email, please see [Microsoft 365 Android instructions](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/add-my-microsoft-365-email-to-samsung-email-on-android-16561), or [Workspace email Android instructions](https://www.godaddy.com/article/4906).

You can set up your new Business Email account on your Android's native Mail app.

**Note:** We've used a Samsung Galaxy 5, for the instructions below. The steps to set up email on your Android device might be a little different depending on your phone make/model, and your network provider. All the settings instructions are correct.

01. Open your email application.
02. If you haven't set up an account, yet, skip to the next step. If you have already set up another account:


    1. Tap the 3 line menu in the top left.
    2. Tap **Manage accounts**.

       ![Tap menu, tap Manage account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/xSdxtqms6c0bXb70CsoGK/2bd738cc3b1968d1e591c5f5872af408/business-email-android-set-up-email-01.png)
    3. Tap **+** to add a new email account.

       ![Tap + to add a new account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/2MrQOE5cGQWccHNZk21MQO/678e18a4a92cfe32be9e4f9c8a2197f1/business-email-tap-plus-add-account-02.png)
03. Tap **Others**.

    ![Tap + to add a new account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3IHrEDtVMabd80onGRvwVc/2d6c3a4b11affca406166ccf3ac9d9c0/business-email-tap-others-03.png)
04. Enter your **Email address**, and **Password**.

    ![Enter email address and password](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/2rtqLAhz5uxEbCJQiHDLaS/64d00fb08443cfeacf74a60129101c44/business-email-enter-email-address-password-04.png)


    **Note:** If you already have an account set up in your phone settings, you may be able to select it on this page.

05. Tap **Next**.
06. Tap **IMAP Account**.

    ![Tap IMAP account](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/5dzz3lJanTqy3jLafB4sji/5da0f517367333b2f54afc08f4a698e3/business-email-tap-imap-account-05.png)
07. Complete, or confirm, the following fields:




    | Field | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | Email address | Your full Business Email address |
    | Username | Your full Business Email address, again |
    | Password | Your Business Email password |
    | IMAP server | The [correct incoming server address for your region, found here.](https://www.godaddy.com/article/24071) |
    | Security type and Port | If you choose **SSL**, enter **993**. If you choose **None**, enter **143**. |
    | IMAP path prefix | Leave this blank |

    ![Enter incoming server settings](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/8gQemPD2OHmT3vp0wbsub/927410d99b9e577162a2d0d4658e047a/business-email-enter-incoming-server-settings-06.png)
08. Tap **Next**.
09. Complete, or confirm, the following fields:




    | Field | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | SMTP server | The [correct outgoing server address for your region, found here.](https://www.godaddy.com/article/24071) |
    | Security type and Port | If you choose **SSL**, enter **465**. If you choose **None**, enter **80**, **3535**, or **25**. |
    | Require sign-in | Make sure this is selected |
    | Username | Your full Business Email address |
    | Password | Your Business Email password |

    ![Enter outgoing server settings](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3tpqlAMLEsERYjryC9a7Ev/bb6b587732a2135fc45a7b018d9c5120/business-email-enter-outgoing-server-settings-07.png)
10. Tap **Next**.
11. Set your **Account options** to your liking, and tap **Next**.

    ![Choose account settings, tap Next](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/6UjLWqytCOLydTddNhZ0r0/a8bf2343d5d73d329dfe4889c938ca13/business-email-set-account-options-08.png)
12. You may be given the option to give the account a name (for your own reference), and to set your display name (which shows to others when they receive your email).
13. Tap **Done with accounts**, to go to your inbox.

## Next step

- Send yourself a test email to make sure everything is set up correctly.
