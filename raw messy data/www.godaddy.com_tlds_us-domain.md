### What are .us domain names?

The **.us domain name** is intended for use by residents of the United States of America. However, it’s not an Americans-only deal. Anyone with a connection to the U.S. may **register .us domains**, including individuals and organizations like:

- Citizens or permanent residents of the U.S. or one of its territories, or someone whose primary residence is in the U.S.
- Organizations incorporated in the U.S. or one of its territories, or those that have a bona fide presence in the U.S.
