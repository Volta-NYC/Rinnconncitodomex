## What is email hosting?

Email hosting manages and stores your email accounts on servers made specifically to handle this type of data. Email hosting unlocks [multiple benefits for having a professional email for your business](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/benefits-of-professional-email-for-business).

Once you learn [how to create an email address with your domain name](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/how-to-use-a-custom-domain-name-for-email), it works by routing emails through secure servers. This provides features like spam filtering, mailbox management, and data backup.

### Email hosting vs. free email providers

If you’re familiar with free email providers like Gmail or Yahoo, you might be wondering why you should pay for something you can get without spending a dime. There’s a number of reasons you should avoid using free email for business:

- **Branding**: Once you find your perfect domain, it becomes an integral part of your brand. Using a professional business email lets you reinforce that brand with every message you send. On the other hand, free email can create a disjointed experience that erodes your brand.
- **Credibility**: When you send messages with a professional email that uses your domain name, people recognize that you’ve significantly invested in your business. That signals trust and credibility, and shows that you aren’t a fly-by-night operation that might disappear.
- **Security**: Free email services do include some security features, but they aren’t nearly as effective as you’ll find with professional business email. That’s key when you’re dealing with sensitive information, like customer data or payment information.
- **Features**: Professional email comes out of the box with capabilities that make it more effective for business use. With this type of email, you can also integrate with other platforms for additional features and robust functionality.

### Difference between email hosting and web hosting

Now that we’ve learned what email hosting is and what it does, you might be asking why not just use the hosting for your website for email. Well, while both services store and manage data, they each perform unique functions.

Like we just covered, email hosting covers functions like spam filtering, mailbox management, and data backup. Web hosting, on the other hand, stores website files on servers and makes them accessible online, allowing users to view a website through the internet.

Think of it like a washing machine and dryer. They sit side by side in a laundry room and handle complementary tasks. But would you put dirty clothes into the dryer and expect good results?

## How to choose the right cheap email hosting

We’ve just covered some email hosting providers you should consider, but this is a choice that should address your individual needs. With that in mind, let’s take a look at the criteria you could use when choosing a provider.

### Key factors to consider

As you evaluate providers for affordable email hosting, be sure to consider the following factors:

- **Custom domain support**: Your professional business email should use the domain you registered for business, not a domain of the provider, which you’ll often see with free services.
- **Storage and message limits**: If you plan on sending and storing larger files, verify that the limits for storage and attachments will be adequate and won’t cause interruptions.
- **Security and spam protection**: These are key. Make sure you have robust security to avoid sifting through junk or, worse, dealing with security issues that affect your business.
- **Collaboration tools**: If you work with a team, collaboration tools like a shared calendar or file sharing make a big difference in a fast-paced work environment with multiple projects.
- **Technical support and uptime**: When you hit a bump in the road, it’s important to have support. Factor in the type of support offered by a provider and when they’re available.
- **Scalability:** Have confidence you’ll be able to easily scale your email. For example, as you grow, you might need to add more mailboxes or increase the storage capacity of your plan.
- **Ease of use**: If possible, take a peek at the user interface, especially on mobile. If you can’t, it’s a good idea to check out reviews to see if there are any common issues with usability.

### Step-by-step selection process

It’s always wise to have a plan before you get started with an important decision. Let’s look at the process for picking your email provider.

#### 1\. List your basic requirements (i.e. number of users, features, budget)

To get started shopping for email hosting, jot down your basic requirements. These should include the number of users, essential features (i.e. spam protection, custom domains), storage needs, and budget. This will help narrow down providers that best fit your needs.

#### 2\. Shortlist providers that meet your criteria

After listing your requirements, research and shortlist email hosting providers that match the needs you just came up with. It’s a good idea to be a bit choosy here, in order to keep your liste manageable.

#### 3\. Compare plans for features, reliability, and support

Take a deeper dive into the offerings from your shortlisted providers. Compare their features, pricing, reliability, and customer support to find the best options that align with your specific needs and preferences.

#### 4\. Read recent reviews and check for red flags

Check out recent user reviews and independent evaluations to get a feel for a provider’s reliability and customer satisfaction. Look out for red flags such as frequent outages, poor support, hidden fees, or security concerns.

#### 5\. Test the provider with a trial or basic plan before committing

Before committing, take advantage of a trial or basic plan to test the provider’s service quality, ease of use, and support. This hands-on experience helps ensure the email plan meets your expectations and business requirements.
