## FAQ

### What is Airo AI Builder and how is it different from a traditional logo maker?

Airo AI Builder is GoDaddy’s conversational AI platform designed to help you launch your idea online, and you can start with your logo. Unlike a standalone logo maker or generator, Airo AI Builder guides you through an end-to-end brand-building experience. Generate a logo, define your visual identity, and build and launch your website or app, all within a single, conversational flow. It's not just a logo tool; it's the starting point for your brand.

### Do I need logo design experience to use Airo AI Builder?

No. Airo AI Builder's conversational, prompt-first workflow is built for everyone, from beginners to seasoned experts. Just describe your idea, and Airo AI Builder handles the heavy lifting, guiding you from a simple description to a polished logo and complete brand assets without any design software or prior experience.

### Is Airo AI Builder free to use?

Yes, you can start building your logo immediately with no credit card required. Airo AI Builder includes 50 free AI credits per month, giving you the ability to prompt, generate, and iterate on logo designs at no cost. Downloading your final logo requires a paid plan.

### How does logo creation work in Airo AI Builder?

Creating a logo with Airo AI Builder is fast and straightforward:

1. **Describe your vision** – Share your business name, what you do, and the style or vibe you want (e.g., classic, modern, bold, playful).
2. **Review your generated logos** – Airo AI Builder produces a selection of brand-ready logo options, including both full-color and black-and-white versions.
3. **See your logo in context** – Your logo is automatically displayed on merchandise and everyday objects so you can visualize it in the real world before committing.
4. **Refine until it's right** – Use conversational prompts to request new variations or adjust style, color, and typography.
5. **Get your full brand kit** – Receive your logo files, color palette, font pairings, and usage guidelines, all in one place.

### What do I need to provide to generate a logo?

You just need to share a few simple details, like your business name, what you do, and the personality you want your brand to have (e.g., bold, traditional, playful, minimalist). Airo AI Builder takes it from there, using your input to generate logo designs tailored to your style and industry.

### Can I customize my logo design after it's generated?

Yes. Logo customization happens naturally within the conversational flow. You can request new variations, adjust the style, refine colors and typography, or take the design in a completely new direction, all through simple prompts. Airo AI Builder offers significantly more flexibility than working from fixed logo templates or a standard logo generator.

### Can I see what my logo looks like before finalizing it?

Yes. As part of the creation process, Airo AI Builder automatically previews your logo on real-world items, like merchandise and branded objects, so you can see exactly how your visual identity will look in practice before you commit.

### Will I receive both color and black-and-white versions of my logo?

Yes. Airo AI Builder generates both full-color and black-and-white logo versions so you’re ready for different use cases, from websites and social media profiles to packaging and print materials.

### What file formats and brand assets are included with my logo?

When you generate a logo, you receive a complete, brand-ready kit that includes:

- Logo files exported in common formats, including vector and png
- Color palette with the specific colors used in your logo
- Font pairings curated to complement your logo design
- Logo usage guidelines to ensure consistent, professional application across all of your brand touchpoints

### Can I create more than one logo?

Yes. You can generate multiple logo options and explore different directions before settling on a final design. This approach can be useful if you're in the early stages of naming a business, managing multiple brands, or refreshing an existing visual identity.

### Can I add a tagline or slogan to my logo?

Yes, as part of the creation and customization process, you can add a tagline, slogan or any additional text into your custom logo design.

### What rights do I have to my logo after I create it?

After creating and downloading your logo, you have full, exclusive rights to use it however you choose, across your website, marketing materials, social media, products, and more, without restrictions.

### Can I trademark my logo?

Yes. Once you’ve finalized your logo, you can apply for a trademark to protect your brand identity. Before filing, it's worth researching whether any similar logos already exist in your industry or trademark databases. We recommend consulting a legal professional to evaluate your logo's eligibility for trademark protection and to guide you through the registration process.
