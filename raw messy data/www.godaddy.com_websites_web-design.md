## How Our Website Design Services Work

We've streamlined the process of getting a professional website. Here’s a clear look at our process from start to finish, ensuring a smooth and collaborative journey.

1. **Schedule your discovery call.** It all starts with a one-on-one consultation with a web design specialist. We’ll discuss your goals and align on your brand identity and design vision to ensure your web design reflects the needs and personality of your business.

2. **Provide your content (or we'll create it).** Share your existing logos, photos, and text with our team. Don't have any content yet? No problem. Every plan includes professional copywriting, and we can also provide high-quality, professional images to bring your site to life.

3. **We design and build your website.** With a clear strategy in place, our design experts get to work for you. Our designers craft a visually stunning layout while our copywriters create compelling content that captures your brand’s voice and speaks to your audience.

4. **Review your draft and provide feedback.** We'll share the first draft of your new website for you to review. This is your chance to share feedback, then we’ll work with you to make the revisions needed to get it just right.

5. **Launch your new website.** After your final approval, we handle all the technical details and publish your site for the world to see. The entire process, from the initial call to going live, typically takes around six weeks.

## GoDaddy Web Design Service FAQ

### How can a custom website help my business grow?

A professional website acts like 24/7 salesperson, brand ambassador, and customer service hub, working to achieve tangible results for your business.

A custom site can:

- Generate business leads. A website works around the clock to capture interest. Through engaging copy, strategic calls-to-action, and contact forms, it can convert visitors into leads or customers, even while you sleep.
- Establish credibility. Customers expect legitimate businesses to have a professional website. A website can build trust by showcasing testimonials and your unique story. A well-designed site signals you are a serious and trustworthy brand.
- Drive sales and revenue. Whether you're selling products through an online store or promoting services, your website serves as a hub for transactions. It supports your sales process by educating customers, answering their questions upfront, and guiding them to make a purchase.

In short, a professional-looking website isn't an expense, it's a strategic investment that engages customers, builds trust in your brand, and provides a clear return.

### What platform or CMS (Content Management System) does GoDaddy Web Design Services build on?

We build all websites on WordPress, the world’s most popular Content Management System (CMS).

We choose WordPress because it offers a combination of flexibility, SEO-friendliness, and scalability. This means your website won't just look great, it will be built on a strong foundation that can grow with your business, integrate with numerous plugins and tools, and give you full ownership of your digital presence.

If you currently have a website on a different platform, like Wix, Squarespace or Weebly, we can work with you to redesign your site while moving you to a more powerful and professional WordPress site.

### Can I give feedback on the web design as it’s being built?

Yes. You will have frequent contact points with your website specialist as the website is being built, and you’ll be asked for feedback on the design. You may contact us via the Website Design Hub, email, or phone.

### How long does the web design process typically take?

Our standard web design process takes between 6 to 8 weeks from the initial consultation to the launch of your new site. This timeline ensures that we have adequate time for design, development, and revisions.

The most important factor for staying on schedule is our collaboration with you. The timeline depends on the prompt delivery of any content you wish to provide (like specific text or images) and timely feedback and approvals during the review stages. Our team is dedicated to making the process efficient and transparent, and you can track progress every step of the way in your project dashboard.

### Will my new website be mobile-friendly and optimized for SEO?

Yes. Both mobile compatibility and SEO are included as part of our Website Design Service.

- **Mobile-friendliness:** Your website will be built using responsive design, meaning it will automatically adapt to look great and work flawlessly on every screen size, from phones to desktops.
- **Search Engine Optimization:** Your design package includes essential on-page SEO. We build your site with an SEO-friendly structure, compelling content and keyword-optimized meta tags so you can be found online by customers.

SEO requires an ongoing effort to maintain and improve organic visibility, so we recommend GoDaddy SEO Services, which can provide continuous optimization to help you outrank your competitors.

### Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, we absolutely can help you with a website redesign. Whether your current site looks outdated, isn't mobile-friendly, is difficult to update, or simply isn’t delivering the results you need, our experts can help. We'll redesign your site on the WordPress platform and work with you to bring your ideas to life online.

### If I'm doing a redesign, what happens to the content on my old website?

We can migrate your existing content (text, images, and blog posts) to your new website. Your initial consultation is the perfect time to discuss which content you'd like to keep, what you want to refresh, and what you want to leave behind. A redesign is a great opportunity to update your messaging, and our professional copywriters are here to help.

### Will I lose my SEO ranking during a redesign?

Protecting your hard-earned search ranking is our top priority. Our process is designed to ensure a smooth transition. We incorporate SEO best practices, including mapping out your old URLs and setting up proper 301 redirects to the new pages. This tells search engines like Google where to find your content, preserving your domain authority, and minimizing any impact on your traffic.
