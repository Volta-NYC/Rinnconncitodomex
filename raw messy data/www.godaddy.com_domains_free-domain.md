## Free Domain FAQ

### How to get a free domain name?

You can get your free GoDaddy domain name when you purchase an annual [Web Hosting](https://www.godaddy.com/hosting/web-hosting) plan or any annual [Managed Hosting for WordPress](https://www.godaddy.com/hosting/wordpress-hosting) plan by GoDaddy and you’ll automatically qualify for a free domain.

Once you click Add to Cart for a hosting or WordPress plan, you’ll be given the option to select monthly or annual terms. Make sure to choose an annual term if you want to get the free domain.

Click Continue with These Options at the bottom of the page to move on to the next step. This is where you’ll see a search bar to look for the domain name you want.

Once you’ve found the name you want, just add the domain to your cart and continue to checkout.

### Can I get a free .com domain name?

Yes. You can get a free .com domain name with the purchase of any annual Web Hosting or WordPress plan from GoDaddy. Although we cannot guarantee that the name you want with a .com domain extension will be available, our nifty domain search technology can help you pick a similar alternative domain name.

### Are there any other costs included?

Yes. There are some additional costs that come with your free domain. You’ll need to purchase an annual plan for either GoDaddy Web Hosting or WordPress hosting to claim your free domain name. Plus an ICANN fee of $0.20 per domain name per year for domain name registration, domain name renewal or domain name transfer. If you purchase a domain name for a term longer than the term of the plan, you will be charged for the additional registration term at the then-current rate. The free domain offer applies only to the initial purchase term. After the initial purchase term, domains purchased through this offer will renew at the then-current renewal price.
