# Edit my domain contact info

Change the details for the underlying contact info on your domains at any time in your GoDaddy account. Eligible domains registered with GoDaddy have [Domain Privacy automatically applied](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-domain-privacy-41145) to hide Registrant contact info on [our public GoDaddy WHOIS directory](https://godaddy.com/whois). This info will be substituted with details from our Domain Privacy partner, Domains By Proxy®, when Domain Privacy is set to **On**.

1. Go to your GoDaddy [Domain Portfolio](https://dcc.godaddy.com/control/portfolio&prog_id=PROG_ID). You might be prompted to sign in.
2. Select an individual domain to access the **Domain Settings** page.

![select a single domain](https://images.ctfassets.net/7y9uzj0z4srt/3fePtruKyjtnYV6gajJMNY/0756bc261c28fac462b4a28a068a9f59/image-domains-11-select-single-domain.png)
3. Select **Edit** next to **Contact Info**.
4. Enter your new contact details.


   - **Note:** Effective August 21, 2025, only Registrant contact details are required and publicly displayed for most domains, depending on registry and [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/consensus-policies/registration-data-policy#registration-data-policy) requirements. Other contact information may be collected but won't be shown in WHOIS records.
5. Select **Continue** to save your edits.


   - Domains with [Domain Protection](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-domain-protection-32311) require identity verification to complete certain actions. If you've had [2-step verification](https://www.godaddy.com/help/enable-or-disable-2-step-verification-for-your-godaddy-account-7502) (2SV) turned on for at least 24 hours, enter the code we sent via SMS, or enter the code from your authenticator app. Otherwise, enter the [one-time password](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-is-a-one-time-password-32250) we sent to the email address on your GoDaddy account.

**Required:** If [Domain Privacy is **Off**](https://www.godaddy.com/help/change-my-domain-privacy-level-32283) and you updated the Registrant **first name**, **last name**, **organization** or **email address**, you'll need to [approve those contact info updates](https://www.godaddy.com/help/approve-my-registrant-contact-updates-24654).

You'll see a Success confirmation that your domains have been updated. The new contact info may take up to 90 minutes to show in your account and [the public WHOIS directory](https://godaddy.com/whois).

## Related step

- Don't forget to [approve any changes](https://www.godaddy.com/help/approve-my-registrant-contact-updates-24654) made to the first name, last name, organization or email address if [Domain Privacy is set to **Limited** or **Off**](https://www.godaddy.com/help/change-my-domain-privacy-level-32283).
