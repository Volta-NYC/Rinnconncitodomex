## Professional Business Email FAQ

### What is Professional Business Email?

Professional Business Email, also known as domain email, allows you to create domain-based email addresses, giving your business a professional and credible appearance. It offers user-friendly features, compatibility with popular email clients, automatic syncing across devices, and strong security to help keep your communications professional and secure.

### What are the benefits of having an email address that has a custom email domain?

A domain-based business email address makes your business look more professional and trustworthy, making it easier for clients to recognize and trust your communications. Each email you send also helps promote your brand.

### Can I set up forwarding with professional email?

Yes, you can [set up forwarding](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/forward-incoming-email-to-another-email-address-32282) to other email addresses with professional email powered by Titan.

### Does the business email include email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)?

Yes, your GoDaddy business email (powered by Titan) will be set up with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC in your DNS settings. These protocols help verify the legitimacy of your emails and protect against spoofing and phishing.

### Can I create a contact group?

Yes, you can email multiple people at once by adding them to a contact group. Sending a message to the group will send it to each of its members, saving you from adding each recipient individually. This feature is only available for Pro Plus, Premium, and Ultra plans.

### How will I access my email?

You can access your email through the GoDaddy [Email & Office Dashboard](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/access-my-email-office-dashboard-26409), via [Webmail](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/sign-in-to-webmail-32291) in your web browser, or by setting it up on your phone, tablet or computer using your [preferred email client](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/set-up-professional-email-on-my-phone-and-computer-32292).

### Does GoDaddy business email come with a mobile and desktop app?

Yes, there is a mobile app available, and you can also set up a Progressive Web App (PWA) on certain web browsers for desktop access.

### Can I add professional email to any email client?

Yes, you can [add your professional email powered by Titan accounts to any email client](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/set-up-professional-email-on-my-phone-and-computer-32292) on your phone, tablet or computer.

### Which languages is GoDaddy Professional Email (powered by Titan) available in?

GoDaddy's help articles and product panels are available in all the languages supported on our website to assist with usage. The main Titan email interface and mobile versions are available in German, English, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Portuguese, Swedish, Turkish, Russian (Ukraine), Chinese Simplified, and Chinese Traditional.

### What is email hosting?

Email hosting allows you to set up a professional, custom email address that matches your website’s domain, like you@yourdomain.com. It also helps keep your emails secure and easily accessible, wherever you are.

By choosing GoDaddy for your professional business email, you can buy without worries as you find the plan that works best, backed by our 30-day money-back guarantee (applicable on annual term purchases). Plus, our award-winning customer support team is available to help you when you need it.
