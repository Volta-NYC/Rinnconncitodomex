## Best payment processing companies FAQ

### How do small businesses process payments?

Small businesses typically accept and process payments by using a payment processing system. Payment processors provide solutions that make it easy to accept a variety of payment methods ― credit and debit cards, and contactless payments with Apple Pay and Google Pay ― in-person, online, and over the phone.

### How should I choose the right credit card processing company?

Sort through payment processor options to know what features your business type requires. The right payment processor will provide flexible, secure payment options that enable you to take payments, so you can meet your customers where they are and grow your business on your terms. For example, if you’re an ecommerce company, a POS system may not be required. Similarly, if you only do in-person business, an online payment portal may not be needed. Hybrid businesses will benefit from providers that connect in-person and online sales so you can manage all your selling in one place, no matter where — or how — customers are buying. Before settling on a provider, don’t forget to evaluate payment processing fees. GoDaddy has the lowest fees compared to other leading credit card processing companies.

### What is the best payment processor for small businesses?

The best payment processors enable small businesses to accept payments in minutes, get paid quickly, and sell online, in-person, and over the phone, securely. With GoDaddy Payments, you get the lowest fees for in-person and ecommerce transactions compared to other leading providers so you can keep more of your money. GoDaddy Payments also comes integrated and is easily enabled with our website solutions, like [GoDaddy Airo](https://www.godaddy.com/airo), to allow small businesses to manage all transactions from a single dashboard. Plus, GoDaddy Guides are available 24x7 to provide support whenever you need it.

### Should I be aware of any hidden costs or additional fees associated with credit card processing companies?

While some payment processing companies like GoDaddy have no hidden fees beyond standard payment processing, others do. Merchants may be responsible for a variety of other fees like PCI compliance costs, statement fees, monthly fees, termination fees, virtual terminal service fees, and recurring billing.

### How can I ensure that my credit card processing company is secure and reliable?

Payment processors like GoDaddy follow strict PCI compliance. That means they’re doing their part to help prevent credit card fraud and keep customer credit card information secure.
