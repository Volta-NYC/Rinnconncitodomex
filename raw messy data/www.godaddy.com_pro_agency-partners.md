## GoDaddy Agency Partner Program FAQ

### How much does the GoDaddy Agency Partner Program cost?

The program is free for qualified web professionals to join.

### What is the GoDaddy Agency Partner Program, and why should I join?

The GoDaddy Agency Partner Program is built to help web designers, developers, and agencies grow their business with exclusive tools, support, and rewards.

Benefits include:

- Access to client referrals
- Dedicated Agency Success Manager
- Priority Support for WordPress
- Up to 20% commission on client purchases
- Co-branded marketing opportunities
- Join to grow faster with a partner that supports your success.

### What are the requirements to join the GoDaddy Agency Partner Program?

To join the GoDaddy Agency Partner Program, you must be a GoDaddy customer using [the Hub 2 by GoDaddy Pro](https://www.godaddy.com/pro/hub). You will also need an agency website showcasing your work and the ability to deliver expert web services and support to GoDaddy customers.

### How is my total spend calculated for tier placement?

Tier spend is calculated using the rolling 12-month revenue from your GoDaddy account and the rolling 12-month revenue from all client accounts where you have delegated access. When you join the program, we’ll factor in your spending to determine your starting tier.

### What kind of customer leads can a GoDaddy Agency Partner expect to receive, and how many can they expect to receive?

We strive to match leads based on your agency's specializations and interests. Leads are provided for new business referrals with no set limit.

### What kind of sales support does GoDaddy offer?

GoDaddy provides personalized sales support to help you close more deals. This includes one-on-one coaching, sales one-pagers, access to product experts, and the option to bring your Agency Success Manager into sales calls for added support and credibility.

### How can an agency leverage existing clients for benefits?

Your Agency Success Manager will help identify opportunities with your current client base to refer additional GoDaddy products for commissions.

### How do commissions work with the GoDaddy Agency Partner Program?

Commissions are tracked through Commission Junction and earned when clients purchase GoDaddy products using your personalized referral link. You’ll receive a percentage of the initial cart value, with payouts issued once a month.

### What kind of agencies are a good fit for the GoDaddy Agency Partner Program?

The program is well-suited for agencies that help others establish or grow their digital presence by offering a variety of web services, such as site design and digital marketing.

### How can I track my performance in the program?

Your performance is based on the rolling 12-month revenue from your account plus the revenue from client accounts where you’ve been granted delegated access. Your Agency Success Manager will share regular updates and help you progress to the next level in the program. We’re also working on adding this tracking directly into the Hub for easier visibility.

### Who can I contact for questions or issues?

For any questions or issues, your Agency Success Team is here to help. New partners get access to a dedicated Slack channel and a support email, making it easy to get answers, share feedback, and stay connected with the GoDaddy team.
