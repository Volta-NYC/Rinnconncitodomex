## Food and Beverage Point of Sale FAQ

### What should I look for in a food and beverage POS software solution?

A POS solution in the food and beverage industry must:

- Be fast, secure, easy to-use and provide excellent customer support
- Accept the payment methods your customers prefer
- Offer WiFi and cellular connectivity, and take offline payments
- Support essential functions like online ordering, order customization, tip management, analytics and reporting
- Integrate with business growth tools like gift card programs, loyalty programs, accounting integration, and business funding

### What should I look for in food & beverage POS hardware?

When selecting POS hardware for your food and beverage business, it’s important to choose a system that matches your specific needs.

For fixed locations, a robust countertop POS terminal with a customer-facing display—like the Smart Terminal Duo—provides a seamless customer experience. If you handle high-volume or complex transactions, a larger device with a swivel screen, such as the Smart Terminal Pro, can help speed up checkout and reduce errors.

For pop-ups or outdoor seating, a compact and lightweight handheld device like the Smart Terminal Flex is a great choice. To get started quickly or for payments on the go, consider using a mobile POS app, such as [Tap to Pay on the Mobile App](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/tap-to-pay).

Smart Terminal Duo and Smart Terminal Flex feature built-in scanners and printers. Plus, all our Smart Terminals are compatible with a wide range of external accessories, including cash drawers, printers, and barcode scanners.

Learn more about the [POS system hardware options](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale/hardware) from GoDaddy.

### What types of food and beverage businesses are GoDaddy's POS solution designed for?

Smart Terminal POS solutions are ideal for independent food trucks, coffee shops, cafés, bakeries, juice bars, donut and bagel shops, and other fast food or quick-serve restaurants that primarily offer counter ordering and pick up.

### How is GoDaddy's Smart Terminal POS different from other POS devices?

Smart Terminal POS uses [GoDaddy Payment Processing](https://www.godaddy.com/payments) which offers the lowest fees in the industry, with no per-transaction fees (2.3% + 0¢). This can mean huge savings for businesses with smaller average order sizes. Also, the intuitive interface uses menu images and easy search options, so even new or seasonal staff can get up to speed quickly, helping reduce training time and errors.

### Can I switch from my existing POS system to a GoDaddy Smart Terminal POS?

Yes, switching is easy. Use our AI-powered menu builder, or bulk import tools to quickly set up a catalog that reflects your menu. Our dedicated support team is available 24/7 and can help you migrate with minimal disruption.

### Can I offer online ordering or QR code ordering for pickup?

Yes, you can easily create an online ordering page directly from your menu, and provide a QR code in-store for customers to order and pick up at the counter. You can also share your ordering link on your website or social media accounts so customers can order ahead and pick up in-store.

### Can I use GoDaddy’s POS solution both in-store and for offsite events?

GoDaddy’s POS solution allows you to sell in-store as well as events from a single dashboard. Using our Smart Terminals or [Tap to Pay on the Mobile App](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/tap-to-pay) makes it easy to process sales anywhere, whether you’re at your brick-and-mortar shop or on the road.

### What if my internet goes down, can I still take orders?

Yes, GoDaddy's Smart Terminals offer offline payments, so you can continue to process orders and accept payments even if your internet connection is temporarily interrupted. The transactions will sync once your device is back online.

### What payment methods are supported by GoDaddy's food and beverage POS solution?

Our Smart Terminals support major credit and debit cards (including EMV chip cards), contactless payments, and digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay.

With GoDaddy Payments you have additional options like sending [branded invoices](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/invoicing), and using Pay Links to accept online payments.

You can also accept keyed-in payments via [Virtual Terminal](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/virtual-terminal) which is helpful in cardless transactions or when accepting payments over the phone.

### How can I manage my kitchen workflow with GoDaddy’s POS solution?

To streamline your kitchen operations, GoDaddy's POS lets you connect multiple printers and send orders directly from your POS terminal to specific stations—such as the drink counter or food preparation area. This ensures faster workflows and reduces the risk of miscommunication.

We also have options for you to connect your POS terminal to a Kitchen Display System (KDS). By subscribing to apps like Fresh KDS, you can replace traditional paper tickets with digital order screens, helping your kitchen staff stay organized and on track.
