Step 4 of the [Set up my Professional Email](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in/help/set-up-professional-email-on-my-phone-and-computer-32292) series.

There are many different options for email apps and email clients that you can use to access your Professional Email or Professional Email powered by Titan email address.

If you’re using Professional Email, select the **Professional Email** tab. If you’re using Professional Email powered by Titan, select the **Titan** tab.
