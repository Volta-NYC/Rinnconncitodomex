## GoDaddy Website Builder FAQ

### What is a website builder? Do I need to know how to code?

A website builder is a tool or a platform that enables users and businesses to create and design their own websites without coding or programming skills. Website builders provide a user-friendly interface pre-designed template, and multiple customization options. The GoDaddy Website Builder (formerly known as GoCentral) is an all-in-one solution perfect for DIY website creators, helping you easily build and launch a professional website or promote a brand or business in minutes.

### Is my website really free?

After you experience the premium features of our paid plans free for 7 days, you will be downgraded to a free version of the Websites + Marketing service. Don’t worry, if you choose not to upgrade you won’t lose the work you’ve already done to your free site and you can upgrade anytime to continue enjoying premium features (such as connecting your domain). You can upgrade your plan in the My Products section of your account or with the help of a GoDaddy Guide. [See Website Builder plans and pricing](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/website-builder/selectplan) to find one that suits your needs.

### How long does it take to create a website with GoDaddy Website Builder?

Create your website using AI in a matter of minutes with GoDaddy Website Builder. Choose your theme and brand colors, create your page-to-page flow, add your content — and your fully-functioning website is complete. From Start to Publish, it’s that easy.

### Can I connect a custom domain to my free website?

During your initial 7-day free trial of premium features you can connect a custom domain to your website. At the end of your trial, your website will be moved to a temporary domain like "www.yoursite.godaddysites.com" unless you upgrade to a paid plan.

### Can I connect my social media and website?

Yes. GoDaddy Website Builder can act as your online hub. The option to link to your Facebook, Instagram, X, Pinterest, LinkedIn and YouTube on your website gives you an advantage in communicating with your visitors. By centrally locating all the links in one place – your new website – you’re giving your online visitors a seamless experience.

### Can I turn my website into an online store?

Yes. With a free website plan, you’ll be able to set up your online store and start taking payments in minutes with GoDaddy Payments. For a full-featured online store with advanced payment options, various shipping methods and increased functionality, you’ll need to upgrade to the Ecommerce plan. Once you’ve upgraded, you can accept orders and build the ultimate shopping experience with features like Apple Pay, highlighted product sections and promotional coupons. Learn more about [Online Store.](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/online-store)

### Are SEO (Search Engine Optimization) tools included with Website Builder?

[Select Website Builder plans](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/website-builder/selectplan) come with built-in SEO tools to help your website show up in search engines like Google. In Website Builder, you’ll find the SEO Wizard, a step-by-step guide that provides suggestions on how to optimize each page of your website by adding relevant keywords and description.

To get further detailed insights on user behavior and for more SEO improvements, you can connect your website to Google Analytics and Search Console to track your traffic, user behavior and understand your site’s presence in Google search.

### How do I create a website?

Creating a website is a relatively small effort that can have a huge impact on your business’ bottom line. Here’s how to start building your website:

1. **Get organized** You can plan ahead by thinking about the info and images you want to include on your website, and how you’ll organize them, such as an About page with your contact details or a Menu page to highlight your best offerings. Some people find mapping out a general structure before building their site helpful. But with GoDaddy Airo, you also have the option to simply answer a few prompts and let AI generate a tailored website draft for you in minutes, no technical skills required.
2. **Choose a template or use our AI to start** Create a site with our [AI website builder](https://www.godaddy.com/airo/ai-builder). It can automatically guide you to the right colors, fonts, and designer-made templates based on your preferences, saving you hours — no coding required. You’re in control: review, edit, and personalize what AI generates as much or as little as you want, saving you hours of manual work.
3. **Customize your website** Use our easy editor to add your content, products and business info, and customize colors, fonts and layouts. GoDaddy’s templates are highly customizable. You can modify layouts, colors, fonts, images, sections, and more using the user-friendly interface. Easily add, remove, or rearrange site elements to achieve your desired look. Quickly add your content, products, and business information with our simple editor.
4. **Choose your domain** Register a custom domain or connect one you already own to your website. This helps people easily identify your business from your site’s online address and protect your brand online.
5. **Optimize your website for search engines** Tap into AI-powered SEO tools through Airo Plus to help your website get found easily in search results, a simple step to boost your online visibility from day one.
6. **Launch your website** Publish your website and watch your business grow, while managing everything (website updates, reviews and social posts) from a single dashboard, on any device.
