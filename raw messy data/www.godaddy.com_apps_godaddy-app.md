## GoDaddy app FAQ

### What is the GoDaddy app?

The GoDaddy app (formerly GoDaddy Studio) lets you create, manage, and grow your business. It combines creative tools with comprehensive business management features like domain sales, website creation, customer communications, marketing campaigns, and more.

### What happened to GoDaddy Studio?

Studio is now the GoDaddy app. All the features and functionality of Studio that you love remain the same, and we've also added some new features and improvements to efficiently manage your business.

### What can I do with the GoDaddy app?

Website and domain management

- Build and design your business website.
- Use our [website maker](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/website-builder) to create and maintain [ecommerce website](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/online-store), portfolio website, personal sites, [link in bio sites](https://www.godaddy.com/link-in-bio), etc.
- Update and monitor your website on the go.
- Search for and manage all your [domains](https://www.godaddy.com/domains) in one place.

Marketing and content creation

- Create logos with our [logo maker](https://www.godaddy.com/logo-maker) and develop branded social posts, photos, and videos with our content tools.
- Access creative tools and templates carried over from GoDaddy Studio.
- Create and schedule social posts across Facebook and Instagram.
- Design professional marketing content to grow your brand.
- Run [email marketing](https://www.godaddy.com/online-marketing/digital-marketing-services) campaigns to reach and nurture customers.

Business management

- Connect with customers through GoDaddy Conversations [Unified Inbox](https://www.godaddy.com/conversations) and related tools.
- Stay informed with notifications for renewals and important account updates.
- Protect your account with multifactor authentication.

### Is the GoDaddy app free?

Yes. You can use the GoDaddy app for free.\* The free version includes standard templates, photos, graphics and font collections. If you’d like to access premium templates and picture editor tools, we’d suggest bundling the GoDaddy app with a website by purchasing the Websites + Marketing Basic plan, which gives you website building and marketing tools, plus unlimited photo editing capabilities with the GoDaddy app. [Check out our Websites + Marketing plans](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/website-builder) (The GoDaddy app is included in all plans).

### Is the GoDaddy app available on iOS and Android?

Yes. The GoDaddy app is available on both iOS and Android devices.

### Which OS versions are supported?

The GoDaddy app supports the latest and recent versions of iOS and Android. For the best experience, we recommend keeping your device up to date.

### Can I use the GoDaddy app on a tablet or iPad?

Yes, the GoDaddy app can be used on tablets, though it’s primarily designed for a mobile-first experience.

### How can the Conversations Inbox be accessed on the app?

The Conversations Inbox can be accessed on the app after subscribing to the Conversations Essentials plan through web or app.
