## LLC Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to register an LLC?

Filing is free plus state fees, which vary by state.

### How long does it take to create an LLC with GoDaddy?

The time it takes to form an LLC depends largely on how quickly your state’s Secretary of State processes business filings, which varies widely by state and time of year. While LegalZoom prepares your formation documents quickly (usually within 1–2 business days) the overall timeline is determined by your state’s processing speed. Many states offer expedited options for an additional fee, which can shorten turnaround times.

### Why should I use GoDaddy's LLC formation services?

We're partnering with LegalZoom, who’ve helped millions of businesses form their LLC quickly and confidently. Customers can expect fast filing, registered agent and compliance support, and the convenience of managing their LLC alongside everything else they use GoDaddy for such as domains, websites, email, etc.

### What do I need to get started?

To file your LLC, you will need:

- Desired business name
- First and Last Name
- Street Address
- Email and Phone Number
- Payment Method

### Can I set up an LLC in any state? Which state should I choose?

Yes, you can form an LLC in any state. Just keep in mind that each state has its own filing and maintenance requirements. You’ll also need a physical address in the state (not a PO box), which is typically handled by a Registered Agent.

### Do I need a lawyer to start an LLC?

A lawyer is not necessary to form an LLC.

### What is a registered agent, and do I need one?

A Registered Agent is a person or company designated to receive legal and tax documents on behalf of an LLC. Every state requires one. If someone doesn't have a physical address in the state where they're forming your LLC, most formation services (i.e., LegalZoom) will provide a Registered Agent. Also, some people choose to hire a Registered Agent even if they live in the state where their business is registered. It allows them to keep their personal address private, maintain a separate official address for legal documents, and get help staying compliant with state requirements.
