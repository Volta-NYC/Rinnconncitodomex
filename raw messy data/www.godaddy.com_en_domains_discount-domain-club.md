## Discount Domain Club FAQ

### What is the Discount Domain Club?

The Discount Domain Club is a membership program designed for businesses that hold larger portfolios of domain names. Members benefit from exclusive wholesale pricing on a wide range of domain extensions, ensuring cost savings across their domain portfolios.

### Which plan is right for me?

There are three membership plans:

- Basic Membership: Ideal for portfolios with up to 24 domain names. This plan offers discounted pricing on 20 popular domain extensions, including .com, .net, .org, .info, and .biz.
- Premium Membership: Suited for portfolios with 25 or more domain names. It provides discounts on 40 domain extensions.
- Domain Pro Membership: For serious domain professionals with larger portfolios. This plan provides discounts on every single extension GoDaddy offers (over 500 total). This tier also includes additional access, tools and discounts for domain professionals to buy, sell, manage and monetize their portfolios.

### How does the Discount Domain Club work?

Upon purchasing a membership, discounts are automatically applied during checkout for new domain registrations, renewals, and transfers. No additional setup is required, and the membership renews annually as long as auto-renewal is enabled.

### What domain extensions are included in the Discount Domain Club?

The Basic Membership covers 20 popular domain extensions, such as .com, .net, .org, .info, and .biz. The Premium Membership extends discounts to 40 domain extensions, and Domain Pro Membership covers over 500 domain extensions.

### How does the Discount Domain Club pricing compare to other promotional pricing?

Discount Domain Club members receive GoDaddy's best pricing on domains. If a promotional price happens to be lower than the Discount Domain Club price, members automatically receive the lower promotional price.

### How do I upgrade or downgrade my membership plan?

To upgrade, cancel your current plan and then purchase your new desired plan. Similarly, to downgrade, cancel your current plan and then purchase your new desired plan.

### What are all the discounts available with Discount Domain Club?

With Discount Domain Club, you get fantastic discounts up to 60% off our everyday domain prices depending on the plan selected.

Here's a breakdown of the discounts

- Basic Plan: Up to 40% off our top 20 TLDs
- Premium Plan: Up to 50% off our top 40 TLDs
- Pro Plan: Up to 60% off every TLD GoDaddy offers

Domain Pro members all receive discounts on select aftermarket services like 75% off Domain Broker Service claims.

### Do the Discount Domain Club prices apply to all domain extensions?

Only the Domain Pro plan provides discounts on every TLD GoDaddy offers, over 500 total.

### What if all my domain names are at another registrar?

That’s not a problem. As a Discount Domain Club member, you also receive discounts on domain transfers. Moving your portfolio to us can save you money and keep all of your valuable assets in one place. For more information about moving your domains to GoDaddy, visit our [Bulk Transfer page](https://www.godaddy.com/en/domains/bulk-domain-transfer).

### After I purchase a Discount Domain Club Membership, what do I need to do?

To enjoy the great discounts Discount Domain Club offers, you don't need to do anything at all. No setup is required. Just sign up and from that point forward all of your domain name purchases and renewals get discounted.

### How do I activate my free Premium CashParking and Auctions memberships included with Domain Pro?

To access your free memberships:

1. Go to the [Premium CashParking page](https://www.godaddy.com/en/domains/cashparking) and and click “Add to Cart” under the Premium CashParking.
2. Go to the [Auctions membership page](https://www.godaddy.com/en/auctions/domain-auctions) and click “Join Now.”
3. You'll see a $0 price for each in cart — just complete the checkout process.

There's no charge, and activation is seamless. Once completed, both memberships will be added to your account and will renew at $0 as long as your Domain Pro membership stays active.
