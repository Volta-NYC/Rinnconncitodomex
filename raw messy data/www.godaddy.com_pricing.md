## GoDaddy plans and pricing FAQ

### Is GoDaddy the largest domain name registrar?

Yes. GoDaddy is the world's largest domain name registrar with more that 80 million domains under management, trusted by over 20 million customers across 100+ countries. For more than 25 years, entrepreneurs, small businesses, and creators have turned to GoDaddy to claim their place online, making us the global leader in domain registration.

### Is GoDaddy cheap?

GoDaddy offers competitive pricing, with domains starting at just a few dollars and regular promotions on popular extensions like .com, .shop, .ai, and .io. And the value goes beyond price: every domain includes free privacy protection, 24/7 customer support, and access to tools that help you build a website, set up professional email, and launch an online store. You’re not just buying a domain; you’re getting what you need to help you grow online.

### Is GoDaddy reliable?

Yes. GoDaddy is backed by enterprise-grade infrastructure and global data centers that keep your domain and website running around the clock. Our award-winning customer support team is available 24/7, so you always have help when you need it. With over two decades of experience and millions of customers worldwide, reliability isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

### Why should I buy my domain from GoDaddy?

Because GoDaddy gives you more than a domain, we give you a head start. Here's what's included:

- The widest selection of domain extensions, from classic .com to specialty options like .ai, .shop, and .store
- Free domain privacy protection to help keep your personal info safe
- Easy domain management with one dashboard for everything you own
- 24/7 expert support
- Transparent pricing with no surprise renewal fees on most TLDs

Whether you're claiming your first domain or your fiftieth, GoDaddy makes it simple to quickly go from idea to online.

### Is GoDaddy an all-in-one solution for small businesses?

Yes, and that's what makes GoDaddy an ideal partner for SMBs. Running a small business means “wearing a lot of hats,” and the last thing you need is a different tool for every task. GoDaddy connects your entire tech stack in one place: register your domain, build your website, set up a professional email address, launch an online store, manage social media content, run email marketing campaigns, and accept payments all from one dashboard.
No more piecing together platforms that don't communicate with each other. Whether you're just starting out or ready to scale, GoDaddy gives small business owners one seamless solution to build, run, and help grow online.
