## Frequently asked questions

### I manage a bunch of sites. Do I have to pay to manage them?

No. With the Hub, manage as many sites as you want for free. You can choose which sites you’d like to use Site Maintenance Pack.

### Once I put a sit on Site Maintenance Pack is it forever?

No. You’re free to toggle the Site Maintenance Pack on your sites as much as you want, as long the number of sites with Site Maintenance Pack don’t exceed the pack’s license limit.

### I have 5 sites with the Professional plan (5 sites) pack. If I add a 6th site, am I required to upgrade to the Agency plan (50 sites)?

No. You can purchase any Site Maintenance Pack that suits your project at any time, such as an individual single-site plan.

### Do the features of Site Maintenance Pack come free with GoDaddy Hub for all hosted sites?

The features of Site Maintenance Pack are included for free to all GoDaddy Hosted Hub subscribers. If you decide to migrate any sites from a competitor to GoDaddy, the Site Maintenance Pack features will be included for free.
