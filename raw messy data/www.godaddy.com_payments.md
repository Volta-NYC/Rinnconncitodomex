## GoDaddy Payments FAQ

### How does payment processing work?

When a customer makes a purchase on your online store or point-of-sale system with a credit or debit card or digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay, the integrated payment processor verifies that the card details are accurate and the funds available for the purchase. If the transaction is approved, the funds are transferred from the customer’s card issuing bank into your bank account, typically within 1-2 days.

### What is GoDaddy Payments and how can it help me accept payments?

GoDaddy Payments is our built-in payment gateway for GoDaddy's commerce tools ( [Online Store](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/online-store), [POS system](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/point-of-sale), [Invoicing](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/invoicing), [Pay Links](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/pay-links), [Tap to Pay on Commerce App](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/tap-to-pay), and [Virtual Terminal](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/virtual-terminal)). With GoDaddy Payments, you can take secure, debit and credit card payments on your website, in-person, over the phone and on the go, while managing all your orders and payments from a single dashboard.

### How soon can I get paid from my sales?

You can get paid as soon as the next business day with GoDaddy Payments. And for a fee of 1.75%, you can also get paid on the same day, including weekends and holidays. [Learn more about Same Day Payouts here.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-are-my-payout-options-42464)

### Why should I choose GoDaddy Payments?

With GoDaddy Payments, setup is quick, pricing is simple and transparent, and you can get paid as fast as the next business day. Payments and business data are kept secure with the strictest PCI compliance standards. GoDaddy Payments is integrated with our website solutions – including [GoDaddy Airo](https://www.godaddy.com/airo) \- and our commerce and point-of-sale solutions. This integration allows you to manage your orders and payments in one place, from a single dashboard with Commerce Home.

Plus, GoDaddy Guides are available 24x7 to provide support when you need it. See a [side-by-side comparison of the best payment processors of 2025 here](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/best-payment-processors).

### Can I take payments without a website?

Yes. You don't need a website to take fast, secure payments. You can use our [Virtual Terminal](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/virtual-terminal), which transforms your mobile phone or computer into a credit card terminal to easily take over-the-phone orders. For on-the-go or in-person, contactless payments, you can use [Pay Links](https://www.godaddy.com/payments/pay-links) to create shareable payment requests and send them directly to customers via social media, text or email. And, with one click, you can transform any pay link into a shareable, printable QR code for customers to quickly scan and pay.

### How do I use GoDaddy Payments to take online payments?

With GoDaddy Payments, you have three ecommerce choices:

- [Start building your online store for free with Websites + Marketing](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/online-store). You use GoDaddy Payments as your payment processor as part of the store setup. Once the store is set up, you can upgrade to a paid eCommerce plan to start selling and accepting payments on your website.
- If you wish to build an online store with WordPress, you can choose GoDaddy's Managed Hosting for WordPress with the WooCommerce plugin installed which has GoDaddy Payments as a built-in payments option.
- If you already have a WooCommerce online store you can add the [GoDaddy Payments plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/godaddy-payments/).

### How do I integrate GoDaddy Payments with my WooCommerce account?

- If you are building a new WooCommerce store, the best way to add GoDaddy Payments is to use our Managed Hosting for WordPress Ultimate plan and add the [GoDaddy Payments plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/godaddy-payments/).
- If you have an existing WooCommerce store, the best way to use GoDaddy as your payments provider is to download and activate the [GoDaddy Payments plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/godaddy-payments/).

### Can I use GoDaddy Payments with my current website outside of GoDaddy?

If you have an existing WooCommerce store hosted by another provider, you can add payments processing by downloading and installing the GoDaddy Payments plugin from [Wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/godaddy-payments/).

### What payment methods are supported by GoDaddy Payments?

With GoDaddy Payments, your customers can pay using all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as HSA and FSA cards. For all Invoicing and Pay Links transactions, you can now also provide your customers the simple, secure option to pay by direct bank transfer with ACH.

### What are the fees associated with using GoDaddy's payment processing services to accept payments?

Please visit our Help Center to [learn more about the fees associated with GoDaddy Payments.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/what-are-the-charges-and-fees-for-godaddy-payments-40617)

### Can GoDaddy Payments be used by businesses based outside of the US?

Yes, GoDaddy Payments is available to businesses based in the United States and Canada.

### How long does it take to get approved with GoDaddy Payments?

Many applications receive instant approval so you can start taking payment immediately. In some cases, we may request additional information where approval can take up to one business day.

### Does GoDaddy Payments offer additional security like fraud protection?

Today we don't offer additional fraud protection services. We monitor transactions to catch unusual activity and notify the merchant of potential fraud. The merchant is still liable for chargebacks.

### Are there any restrictions on who can use GoDaddy for payment processing?

Yes, there are categories of businesses and business practices that are restricted from using GoDaddy Payments. [Please see here for more information.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/prohibited-businesses-with-godaddy-payments-40682)

### How does Rate Saver work?

Rate Saver is an opt-in feature available on the Point of Sale Plus plan and Invoicing & Pay Links Plus plan. It allows you to save even more on payment processing fees. With Rate Saver, you can add a surcharge to credit card transactions and benefit from lower debit card fees. [Learn more.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/enabling-rate-saver-41781) Rate Saver applies to transactions on the Smart Terminal, Card Reader, Invoicing, Online Pay Links, Virtual Terminal, and the GoDaddy Commerce App. Your customers will be automatically notified of credit card surcharges during checkout and on receipts.

For more information, please refer to our [Surcharging Guide.](https://www.godaddy.com/help/credit-surcharging-guide-for-merchants-41822)
