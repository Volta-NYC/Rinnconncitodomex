## Domain Auctions FAQ

### What is a domain auction?

GoDaddy's domain auction space serves as a marketplace for expired domains. When a domain expires, it typically goes right back to the registry. If it's a valuable domain, however, registrars will try to sell it through auction, and buyers are always eager to capitalize on potential deals. Participating in a domain auction can be a nuanced business, so [take a look at our Auctions FAQ page](https://www.godaddy.com/help/a-890) for more details on the process.

### Why should I use GoDaddy to buy domains?

GoDaddy offers a variety of benefits when you choose to buy a domain with us. We have a unique Auction [bidder verification process](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/news/godaddy-auctions-introduces-new-domain-extensions) that safeguards against fraudulent activity. We also offer a handy Investor mobile app for [iOS](https://www.godaddy.com/help/a-20069) or [Android](https://www.godaddy.com/help/a-20070) that allows you to watch auctions in real time and view their history (including number of bidders and how much they bid), all from the convenience of your smartphone.

### Do I need a membership to participate in GoDaddy’s domain auctions?

Yes, you'll need to purchase a GoDaddy Auctions® membership for $4.99/year before you can participate in an auction.

### How do I join the auctions community and what are the member benefits I receive?

To join, you'll need to purchase a GoDaddy Auctions membership for $4.99 a year. Once you've purchased a membership, you can take advantage of member benefits such as our [Auctions Tools](https://auctions.godaddy.com/), which can help you prepare before buying any domains.

### How do I sell my domain?

So, it's time for you to sell your domain. Here are the simple steps to pricing, listing and selling your domain through GoDaddy.

**1\. Find out what your domain is worth.**

You'll want to set a realistic price. Often, people list their domains for a price higher than someone is willing to pay. One place to find out the sale prices of comparable domains is free [GoDaddy Domain Value Appraisal Tool.](https://www.godaddy.com/domain-value-appraisal)

The GoDaddy Domain Value and Appraisals tool uses machine learning to predict the value of your domain. It uses mass quantities of data and word tokenization to produce a model that can assess a domain's value.

Just type in the domain you want to sell and you'll learn the estimated value, and well as comparable domains sold. Even better, it’s all free.

**2\. Make communication easy.**

Be sure to [expose](https://www.godaddy.com/help/a-32283) your contact information on [WHOIS](https://www.godaddy.com/whois). That way, when an interested buyer looks up your domain on WHOIS, they can find your publicly available information and contact you directly.

**3\. Put out a "For Sale" sign.**

If you haven’t already, set up a single-page website on your domain, called a park page or domain parking. This page lets visitors know that the domain is owned and for sale. You can [learn more about park pages](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/what-is-domain-parking) and the benefits of having advertising on that page. It’s the simplest way to announce your domain is for sale. But to announce it far and wide, you’ll want to list your domain on some online marketplaces.

**4\. List your domain on online marketplaces.**

List your domain name on Afternic. It’s “the big name” in after-market sales and is a platform with a vast audience. [Learn more about selling your domain on Afternic.](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/tips-for-how-to-sell-your-domain-name-on-afternic)

You can also link your Afternic account with your GoDaddy account so you can manage your sales from inside your account.
